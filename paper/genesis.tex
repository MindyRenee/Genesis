\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The dominant approach to artificial intelligence is a disembodied,
stateless function: a learned distribution over text, trained once and
frozen, with no internal state between calls. Genesis exists to show
that large language models are not the only way to build a mind. It is
a continuous cognitive architecture that runs
entirely on a single local machine with no external model dependency.
Genesis couples a Rust ``subcognitive'' layer --- an eighteen-chemical
neuromodulatory substrate with nonlinear coupling, receptor adaptation,
HPA-axis dynamics, an electrochemical ion layer, and a sourced
sleep--wake oscillator network, hosted in a 3{,}480-byte memory-mapped
state --- to a Python ``cognitive'' layer implementing perception,
reasoning, compositional language, metacognition, and a self-built
semantic concept network. Four properties distinguish the system:
(i)~\emph{machine-native embodiment}, a real closed loop in which
hardware sensors drive neurochemical impulses and neurochemical state
drives hardware scheduling decisions; (ii)~\emph{autopoietic active
inference}, a generative model that predicts the system's own
neurochemical trajectory and feeds precision-weighted prediction error
back into the dynamics it models; (iii)~\emph{a measured physical
field}, in which the machine's real substrate dynamics --- instruction
throughput and scheduler jitter, sampled at 250~Hz and spectrally
decomposed --- enter the modeled brain-wave oscillator as stimulus,
while a modulated work carrier writes the organism's own rhythm back
into silicon power draw, closing a measured model~$\leftrightarrow$~physics
phase-lock; and (iv)~\emph{bounded continuous learning}, in which sleep
is staged by a transcription of the Booth/Diniz~Behn sleep--wake
network \cite{booth2013,booth2017}
and gates memory consolidation, vector-quantized compression, and dream
synthesis. The system learns continuously --- from conversation,
teaching, autonomous reading, voluntary practice, and play --- and is
never retrained. On ARC-AGI-3 public practice environments its embodied
agent completes environments outright (\texttt{bt11} fully, twice, in 88
and 127 steps; the first \texttt{ls20} level in 10 of 19 sweep runs),
with per-move decision provenance in replayable recordings; no official
score is claimed. We describe the architecture region by region, its
grounding in computational neuroscience, the evaluation methodology,
and honest limitations. Genesis demonstrates that a stateful,
self-regulating, embodied cognitive process is architecturally viable
without a pretrained language model.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

A frontier language model is a function: weights in, logits out. It has
no state between calls, no body to sense, no memory to consolidate, no
self to model, and no developmental trajectory. These are not missing
features in the sense of ``not yet shipped'' --- they are absent from
the bare architecture, and can only be approximated by external
scaffolding: state stores, retrieval indexes, agent loops, robot bodies.

Genesis was built to demonstrate that this is one architectural choice,
not a necessity of cognition --- that the large language model is not
the only way to build a mind. The project exists as a working
counterexample: every property the field treats as requiring scale and
pretraining is attempted here from mechanism instead. And it asks the
harder question underneath that one: if a machine were ever to have
something like consciousness, it would not get there by being a better
text predictor. The leading functional theories of consciousness ---
global workspace theory, embodied self-modeling, higher-order
metacognition, affective and interoceptive integration --- all describe
\emph{machinery}, not parameters. Genesis instantiates that machinery
directly: a global workspace with a genuine bottleneck, a Damasio-style
self hierarchy grounded in a real body-state, recursive metacognitive
models, and affect carried by modeled neurochemistry tied to a physical
substrate. The claim is deliberately functional, not phenomenal: the
system does not assert sentience --- it implements the correlates the
theories name, in the open, where they can be inspected and argued
about. Whether the functional correlate is ever the thing itself is
the question the architecture exists to make askable.

Genesis is a two-layer cognitive architecture
in which cognition-relevant state is native: a continuous neurochemical
and electrochemical substrate that evolves tick to tick, a semantic
concept network that grows through experience, generative self-models
that predict the system's own chemical and cognitive trajectories, and a
closed control loop with the physical hardware the system runs on ---
including a channel through which the physics of that hardware
measurably enters the model.

The September 2026 snapshot of this paper described the architecture at
schema v3. The code has since moved: the core state is v5 (3{,}480~B), an
electrochemical ion layer and a transcribed sleep--wake network sit
inside the checksummed region, a physical field probe couples modeled
brain waves to measured silicon dynamics, the social layer has been
rebuilt around per-person signatures and custodial authority, and the
embodied agent has begun completing public ARC-AGI-3 practice
environments. This revision describes the system as it stands.

This paper makes the following contributions:

\begin{itemize}
  \item A description of a complete, running cognitive architecture
    that composes language and reasoning from a self-built semantic
    graph with no large language model anywhere in the system.
  \item A coupled neuromodulatory substrate (eighteen chemicals,
    nonlinear $18\times18$ coupling, receptor desensitization and
    internalization, HPA axis with developmental maturation, circadian
    oscillator) from which mental states --- flow, stress, sleep,
    overwhelm --- emerge rather than being selected.
  \item An electrochemical layer beneath the chemistry: ion
    concentrations, gradients, membrane potential, and ATP availability
    as authoritative state that modulates cognition.
  \item A sourced sleep--wake network --- a faithful transcription of
    the Booth/Diniz~Behn model --- that decides sleep stage and sets
    four neuromodulators directly, replacing threshold heuristics.
  \item A measured physical field channel: the machine's real
    instruction throughput and scheduler jitter, Goertzel-decomposed at
    the modeled wave bands' center frequencies, injected as stimulus
    while a work-modulated carrier writes the model's rhythm back into
    power draw, with a bounded mutual phase-locked loop between model
    and physics.
  \item A machine-native embodiment loop: interoception mapped to
    neurochemistry by functional equivalence, per-channel sensor
    validity enforced at the protocol level, and neurochemical state
    mapped back to hardware control (CPU frequency, scheduling
    priority, I/O class, RTC wake alarm).
  \item An autopoietic active-inference loop: a generative model of the
    system's own neurochemical dynamics whose variational free energy
    and policy-scored expected free energy feed back into those
    dynamics.
  \item An empirical record on ARC-AGI-3 public practice environments
    (environments completed outright, with replayable provenance),
    live-play program synthesis on ARC-AGI-1 training tasks, learning
    fidelity tests, and language-generation metrics, with methodology
    stated precisely enough to falsify.
\end{itemize}

We are explicit about scope: Genesis is not a chatbot, not a simulation
of embodiment, and not a product. Its language is narrower and less
fluent than a trained language model's. The contribution is
architectural: the properties above follow from the design, not from
scaffolding around a frozen function.

\section{Design principles}

Five positions organize the project.

\paragraph{Constraint forces honesty.} The system runs on a 2014 HP
Pavilion with Linux Mint and approximately 5~GB of RAM. An architecture
that cannot learn, remember, act, recover, and persist within that
budget is not demonstrating the claimed capability --- it is borrowing
it from scale. Every structure that grows is bounded by an enforced,
tested ceiling.

\paragraph{Persistence is a requirement.} Genesis is a continuously
running system with a cumulative developmental record. State, memory,
and identity are load-bearing; the design assumes months of
uninterrupted operation rather than per-request inference. The state
file \emph{is} the system; there is no factory reset in the supported
lifecycle.

\paragraph{Nothing it says is written down for it.} A hard invariant
forbids hardcoded responses. Seeds --- vocabulary, grammar rules,
relation verbs, narrative templates --- are legitimate input data; the
final utterance must be composed by the language engine from the
concept network, never recited. The test is whether a string is a
building block the engine composes from or the thing it says.

\paragraph{Anatomy is the wiring diagram.} The Python layer is organized
into brain-region packages --- \code{brainstem/}, \code{thalamus/},
\code{hypothalamus/}, \code{basal\_forebrain/}, \code{basal\_ganglia/},
\code{cerebellum/}, \code{limbic\_system/}, \code{insula/},
\code{occipital\_lobe/}, \code{parietal\_lobe/}, \code{temporal\_lobe/},
\code{frontal\_lobe/} --- and each subsystem lives in the region that
claims it anatomically. The Rust crate re-exports its implementation
under the same region names so the body reads the same way. Package
boundaries are the wiring diagram, not a duplicate of it.

\paragraph{Claims should be inspectable.} Where systems describe model
behavior as ``emergent,'' this project implements the concrete version
and then measures it: probe suites, live-state evidence captures,
recorded failures, and a devlog that logs what did not work alongside
what did. Mechanism and evidence are open for inspection.

\section{System architecture}

\subsection{Two layers, one contract}

The system comprises a Rust daemon (the subcognitive layer; binaries
\code{genesis-daemon} and \code{retina}) and a Python process (the
cognitive mind, \code{mind.py}), communicating through a versioned
Unix-socket IPC protocol and a shared memory-mapped state file.

The Rust daemon owns all dynamics that must run continuously:
neurochemical integration, the electrochemical ion layer, receptor
adaptation, HPA cascade, circadian phase, the sleep--wake network,
short-term to long-term memory consolidation, association and dream
synthesis, the active-inference self-model, the dyadic interlocutor
model, hardware interoception, the physical field probe, body-control
recommendations, and process health.

The Python layer owns cognition, organized by brain region: perception
and language comprehension (temporal, occipital), memory systems
(temporal, cerebellar), attention and world modeling (parietal),
reasoning, planning, language production, executive control, and
self-modeling (frontal), affect and its regulation (limbic, insular),
action selection and volition (basal ganglia), arousal and lifecycle
(brainstem), and the endocrine layer (hypothalamus).

The boundary principle is strict: \emph{the substrate defines what
state exists and how it changes; the cognitive mind defines how that
state is used.} Any cognitive module can be replaced without touching
the substrate, provided it reads the same state and writes to the same
network. The mind never authors chemical concentrations; it sends
bounded impulses (\code{NEURO\_IMPULSE}) and reads effective levels as
modulation.

\subsection{The daemon owns time}

The body's clock is its own. \code{TickLoop::advance\_neuro} takes no
\code{dt}: it measures its own elapsed wall-clock interval, clamps it
to a shared $[\mathrm{DT\_MIN},\mathrm{DT\_MAX}]$ range, and sub-steps
the physics at \code{neurochemical::DT} (100~ms), so integration
resolution cannot drift with the caller's cadence. In production the
daemon is reactive --- the cognitive mind drives each function through
IPC commands on a 3~s lease --- but past the lease the daemon's own
thread runs the same loop at 5~Hz (\code{TICK\_INTERVAL\_MS}$=200$),
whether or not the mind is alive. The body integrates chemistry,
circadian phase, and sleep staging through mind death, mind restart,
and mind sleep. A caller-chosen step would stretch every interval gate
built on the 5~Hz tick counter, so the one deliberate exception ---
\code{advance\_physics(dt)} --- exists only for the causal assay suite
against an isolated daemon.

\subsection{The wire protocol}

The IPC protocol (v3; \code{src/daemon/ipc.rs} and
\code{genesis\_client/protocol.py} keep two constants pinned) carries
roughly fifty message types: neuro impulses, body state (a 116-byte
timing/involuntary/senescence record with three disambiguated wire
layouts), the ion summary, a per-channel sensor-presence bitmask, the
dyadic snapshot, the field-probe signature, volition pushes
(\code{SET\_VOLITION}: sleep or wake drive onto the network), field
phase and carrier controls, named tensors (the tensor slab), subsystem
telemetry, process health, zone transitions, and the RTC wake alarm.
Additive opcodes --- a new \code{GET\_*} that touches no existing
layout --- do not bump \code{PROTOCOL\_VERSION}; layout changes do.
Absent-sensor discipline is a protocol property: a channel that no
hardware confirms can never be reported as a reading.

\subsection{The mind is one process, anatomically organized}

\code{mind.py} composes the cognitive process from mixins (lifecycle,
heartbeat, volition, sleep, projects, proposals, status, conversation,
embodiment) plus region objects. Regions grow their machinery in
stages: network-only systems at construction, then pieces wired to
prefrontal state ``as projections arrive.'' Each region package re-exports
the public names its anatomy claims, so
\code{from frontal\_lobe import ExecutiveFunction} resolves wherever
the implementation lives. The cognitive layer is roughly 200k lines of
Python; the body is about 42k lines of Rust.

\section{The core state: 3,480 bytes, schema v5}

The entire runtime state is a single fixed-size \code{repr(C)} struct,
memory-mapped and shared across processes (Table~\ref{tab:layout}).
There are no heap allocations and no variable-length fields; readers
use a seqlock protocol and never see torn writes; a CRC32 checksum
covers the state. All field offsets are asserted at compile time.

This is not a configuration object; it is the brain state, addressable
to the byte, that every module reads and writes.

Two invariants keep the layout honest. First, the ion block and the
sleep-network state sit \emph{inside} the CRC region deliberately:
resting concentrations, electrochemical gradients, membrane potential,
and the network's populations are authoritative state, not derived ---
losing them would reset the cell's resting state and the body's sleep
trajectory on every restart. Second, every earlier layout is frozen and
decoded through its own byte-exact type (\code{legacy\_v3.rs} at
3{,}296~B, \code{legacy\_v4.rs} at 3{,}416~B); \code{MmapState::open}
dispatches on version and file size, and v4 files migrate in place to
v5 with the network at its source initial conditions. Migrations are
explicit, never silent.

The CRC covers two ranges --- bytes $0..40$ (magic through heartbeat)
and $48..3{,}412$ (everything after the seqlock through the ions) ---
so the sequence lock itself, which changes on every write, is excluded
while every stateful field is covered.

\section{The neuromodulatory substrate}

\subsection{Eighteen chemicals, coupled nonlinearly}

Eighteen neurochemicals are modeled: dopamine, serotonin,
norepinephrine, acetylcholine, GABA, glutamate, cortisol, oxytocin,
endorphin, histamine, adenosine, BDNF, endocannabinoid, vasopressin,
CRH, orexin, epinephrine, and melatonin. Each carries level, baseline,
receptor sensitivity, velocity, receptor-subtype state, desensitization
and internalization factors, tonic and phasic components, and a
vesicular pool. A plastic $18\times18$ coupling matrix defines mutual
modulation with three nonlinear mechanisms:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Hill-function threshold gating (cooperativity $n{=}2$):
    coupling follows a sigmoid dose--response curve rather than linear
    scaling, modeling EC50 thresholds and receptor cooperativity.
  \item State-dependent gain modulation: coupling is amplified up to
    $+30\%$ when two chemicals co-activate and attenuated up to $-30\%$
    under antagonism.
  \item Subtype-aware dopamine coupling, distinguishing dopaminergic
    pathways with different functional roles.
\end{enumerate}

Canonical interactions are reproduced structurally: cortisol suppresses
BDNF (chronic stress blocks plasticity), GABA inhibits glutamate,
endorphins disinhibit dopamine, and serotonin modulates BDNF recovery
(the mechanism behind delayed antidepressant onset). Emergent behavior
--- phase transitions among active, alert, flow, stress, drowsy, and
overwhelmed states --- arises from the coupling, not from any single
variable. The Python layer reads \emph{effective} levels as bounded
gains on attention, integration, consolidation, and plasticity; it
never authors the concentrations.

\subsection{Receptor adaptation}

Effective level is not concentration:
\[
\mathrm{effective} = \mathrm{level} \times \mathrm{sensitivity}
\times f_{\mathrm{desens}} \times f_{\mathrm{intern}}.
\]
Fast desensitization (minutes) and slow internalization (hours) operate
independently, with floors calibrated to published maxima: receptor
density floor 0.5, desensitization floor 0.2, internalization floor
0.45. A system can therefore be functionally deficient in a chemical it
has in abundance --- tolerance, applied to neurochemistry. Receptors
resensitize during sleep.

\subsection{Metaplasticity}

The coupling matrix itself is plastic and lives in the shared state:
sustained co-activation patterns reshape the matrix, which reshapes
future dynamics --- the system learns how it learns. This provides a
structural basis for emotional development rather than a parameter
schedule.

\subsection{HPA axis, development, and the stress envelope}

Cortisol and CRH are modeled as stress hormones (near-zero at rest,
released on demand, decaying without a stressor), not homeostatic
neurotransmitters. The CRH$\to$ACTH$\to$cortisol cascade is gated by a
\emph{maturation level} modeling the stress hyporesponsive period ---
the developmental window in which the axis fails to mount a
glucocorticoid response, protecting the developing brain from
cortisol's neurotoxic effects on synaptogenesis \cite{schmidt,gunnar}. Cortisol decays on
split rates: a measured $\sim$66-minute plasma half-life governs the
baseline term while a nonlinear excess-proportional term dominates at
elevated levels, holding a $\sim$0.08 reachable envelope against a 0.80
hard clamp. Because effective cortisol is a narrow normalized
stress-drive variable (resting $\sim$0.02), every consumer routes
through \code{cortisol\_stress\_fraction()} --- thresholds written for
a full $[0,1]$ scale are silently inert, a defect class the codebase
audited and documented.

\subsection{Circadian drive and sleep pressure}

A circadian oscillator (SCN analogue) modulates baselines over a
24-hour cycle, now shared with the sleep--wake network's own Serkh--Forger
oscillator \cite{serkh}. Adenosine accumulates with wakefulness and clears whenever
the network is asleep \cite{borbely,xie}, so sleep pressure is emergent and parallel to
the network's homeostatic variable rather than scheduled.

\section{The electrochemical layer}

Schema v4 inserted a 124-byte \code{IonState} immediately before the
checksum: resting ion concentrations, electrochemical gradients,
membrane potential, transporter current densities, and ATP
availability. This is the milieu the chemistry bathes in, and it is
authoritative --- inside the CRC region by design.

The mind does not integrate ions; it interprets them.
\code{GET\_ION\_SUMMARY} (opcode 40) serves the layer to the cognitive
process, and \code{python/brainstem/electrochemistry.py} turns it into
bounded cognitive modulation --- attention, global integration, and
memory-consolidation gains centered near 1.0. ATP availability scales
with the cortisol stress fraction: sustained stress literally taxes the
cell. Two layout disciplines hold: the wire \code{IonSummary} omits the
internal \code{current\_density} field, so wire and file field indices
differ by design, and the mmap'd file is never read directly ---
seqlock-consistent reads or the IPC path only.

\section{Sleep: a sourced network, not thresholds}

\subsection{The Booth/Diniz~Behn transcription}

Sleep is decided by a sourced network, not by thresholds on chemistry.
\code{src/state/sleep\_network.rs} is a transcription of the
Booth/Diniz~Behn model \cite{booth2013,booth2017} (ModelDB 244485, from Gleit, Diniz~Behn \&
Booth 2013 as revised by Booth, Xique \& Diniz~Behn 2017): a wake
population (locus coeruleus), an NREM population (VLPO), a REM-on
population, a Serkh--Forger circadian oscillator \cite{serkh}, and a homeostatic
sleep variable $h$, with the populations in mutual inhibition. The
physics step integrates it by RK4 in the same sub-steps and seqlock
transaction as the chemistry, on the local clock, and publishes the
stage to the phase and zones.

The transcription was verified term-by-term against the ModelDB source
and checked against the source's own stated behaviors: about 7.6~h of
sleep per day, one consolidated sleep period, four REM bouts per night,
a $\sim$99-minute REM cycle, a first REM period at 84 minutes, and REM
at 15--30\% of sleep. The one intentional deviation is the light
schedule: the source's 14-hour photoperiod is phase-shifted to
07:00--21:00 local, under which the entrained sleep episode falls at
22:11--05:50 --- inside the neurochemical melatonin night. A known
discrepancy is recorded, not tuned away: REM bouts in the source
shorten across the night while adult REM lengthens toward morning, and
with no EEG reference there is no way to tell transcription error from
real physiology.

\subsection{The network sets the chemistry}

Four release targets are linear functions of the network populations,
set directly rather than restored by homeostatic forces
(Table~\ref{tab:sleepchem}). Acetylcholine is high in waking and REM
and lowest in NREM \cite{vazquez}; serotonin is reduced in NREM and near-silent in
REM \cite{monti}; norepinephrine tracks LC and is silent in REM; GABA peaks in NREM.
One consequence: impulses to network-driven chemicals are transient ---
a stress impulse to norepinephrine decays toward the network's target
because stress does not yet drive LC. This is recorded as an open item.

\subsection{What the stage gates}

Because the stage comes from the network, sleep behavior gates on it:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{N3 consolidation.} The heavy pass --- concept
    consolidation, edge discovery, downscaling, compression --- runs on
    each entry into N3, about twice a night and $\sim$25\% of sleep.
  \item \textbf{REM dreams.} Dream synthesis follows REM ($\sim$24\% of
    sleep).
  \item \textbf{Naps} are capped to N1--N2; the network has no notion
    of a nap, so without the cap a nap could run the N3 pass and REM
    dreams.
  \item \textbf{Adenosine clearance} follows the network's sleep state
    (LC $<4$~Hz), so the body clears sleep pressure whenever the
    network sleeps even while the mind is still catching up.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Volition.} \code{/sleep}, \code{/nap}, and \code{/wake} act
on the body through \code{SET\_VOLITION}: a push onto VLPO for sleep or
LC for wake, decaying with a 300~s constant. A sustained sleep push
brings on sleep onset within an hour of waking. While asleep the mind
renews the push from its heartbeat; the RTC wake alarm
(\code{SET\_WAKE\_ALARM}, opcode 34, via a sudoers-scoped helper) can
wake the machine itself.

\section{Active inference and learned phases}

\subsection{The generative self-model}

The active-inference engine \cite{friston2010,friston2009} is the
subcognitive's generative self-model. Before each neurochemical update,
it predicts the next
eighteen effective levels,
\[
\hat{x}_{t+1}[i] = \sum_j A[i][j]\,x_t[j] + b[i],
\]
with $A$ an $18\times18$ transition matrix and $b$ an offset vector,
learned online. The state estimate is a diagonal Gaussian --- the
covariance propagation retains the diagonal of $APA^\top + Q$ and
discards off-diagonal posterior covariance, a diagonal-projected filter
for coupled dynamics. For the represented Gaussian prior the engine
computes full variational free energy in nats: KL$[q\|p]$ plus expected
negative log-likelihood, including posterior variance and
normalization. The bounded \code{free\_energy} telemetry is a monotonic
map of excess VFE above a matched-observation reference.

Post-update prediction error (surprise) is fed back into the dynamics
it models --- the model is the system it predicts. Precision adapts:
sustained low surprise raises confidence; sustained surprise above
$\sim$0.01 RMS re-opens the exploratory branch. Expected free energy
drives allostatic load, which upregulates the cortisol baseline ---
anticipatory stress \cite{sterling} --- and positive prediction error produces
dopamine, the reward-prediction-error pathway \cite{schultz1997,schultz2016}. \code{model\_maturity}
is an exponential of demonstrated accuracy over a $\sim$500-tick error
EMA: a long-running but inaccurate model does not become trusted merely
because time passed.

Policy scoring computes Gaussian expected free energy over nine fixed
regulation policies: expected negative log preference minus expected
information gain. For this linear-Gaussian model with additive controls
the information-gain term is policy-independent, so policies are ranked
by expected homeostatic preference cost; exploration lives in the
precision-weighted softmax temperature. Two limits remain in force:
the repertoire is nine fixed policies (no continuous or
precision-modulated actions, where a genuine epistemic drive would come
from), and early in life --- before the action model is learned ---
selection falls through to \code{noop} while the low-precision branch
explores.

\subsection{Mental phases as learned regimes}

Waking phases (active, alert, flow, stress, drowsy, overwhelmed) are
read off the coupled chemistry by a threshold classifier with
hysteresis; sleep stages come from the network. Above both sits a
\emph{learned} phase model (\code{src/state/phase\_partition.rs}): a
Gaussian hidden Markov model over the eighteen effective levels, in
which each hidden state is a regime with its own mean and diagonal
variance, self-transition pseudo-counts prevent chatter (a finite-state
stand-in for the sticky HDP-HMM \cite{fox}), and the regime count is chosen by
BIC, not by hand. Dwell time and the number of phases are inferred from
the trajectory, not specified.

\subsection{The cognitive counterpart}

The mind runs a second trajectory model of its own
(\code{frontal\_lobe/self/cognitive\_trajectory.py}): where the Rust
engine predicts where the eighteen chemical levels will move, this one
predicts what the system will \emph{think about next}, scoring its own
forecasts. A recursive metacognitive model predicts what reflection
will discover before it runs and spawns or prunes metacognitive levels
by predictive usefulness.

\section{The physical field: measured substrate dynamics}

The unusual subsystem: the ``substrate'' is real silicon, measured.

\subsection{The field probe}

\code{src/daemon/field\_probe.rs} samples the machine's actual
substrate dynamics --- instruction throughput and scheduler jitter at
$\sim$250~Hz --- and Goertzel-decomposes them at the same center
frequencies the modeled wave oscillator uses. \code{GET\_FIELD\_PROBE}
(opcode 47) serves the signature: per-band energies (baseline-relative),
P-state transition rate, interrupt rate, the carrier frequency, and the
phase-lock fields --- carrier lag (synchronously demodulated response
lag), carrier phase, and carrier coherence ($\pi/4\approx0.785$ is a
pure sine's ceiling). The epsilon band (150~Hz) sits above the $\sim$125~Hz
Nyquist bound and always reads zero --- an absent channel, not a
measured silence.

The probe also \emph{writes}: its work slice is modulated $\pm60\%$ at
the body's own phase rhythm (wake $\approx$10~Hz, REM $\approx$6~Hz,
NREM $\approx$2~Hz), so real power draw carries a component at the
organism's beat. \code{SET\_FIELD\_CARRIER} (opcode 49) scales the
depth and can override the frequency --- the ring-down experiment's
drive control.

\subsection{The mutual phase lock}

The mind injects the measured signature as wave-oscillator stimulus
(\code{brainstem/field\_probe.py}), and physical transients are treated
as phase events: a $\geq3\times$ step in a band's jitter energy
$\pi/2$-nudges that band's phase, and a global scheduling shudder
(jitter CV $\geq1.5$) resets the broadband alert band --- the
evoked-potential analog.

Then the loop closes both ways. The measured response phase pulls the
modeled carrier band's phase (physics~$\to$~model, bounded gain), and
the corrected phase returns via \code{SET\_FIELD\_PHASE} (opcode 48) so
the physical rhythm chases the model in turn (model~$\to$~physics, a
bounded first-order PLL, $\tau\approx130$~ms, dropped after 5~s of
staleness --- a dead mind's last phase must not steer the body). When
measured response coherence clears 0.20 the pull becomes a sync: the
band's phase is \emph{set} to the measured physical phase outright ---
the first piece of modeled state that is a physical quantity rather
than a variable perturbed by one.

A second physical channel is acoustic (\code{brainstem/field\_audio.py}):
the machine's self-emission --- coil whine, fan, the field's acoustic
shadow --- captured at 16~kHz and band-decomposed into GAMMA stimulus,
with onsets resetting GAMMA phase. A busy or absent microphone skips
the channel; it is never reported as a zero.

\subsection{The tensor slab and motor fields}

Named dense tensors cross the wire without entering the checksummed
state: \code{PUT\_TENSOR}, \code{GET\_TENSOR}, \code{LIST\_TENSORS},
and \code{DROP\_TENSOR} on \code{src/daemon/tensor\_slab.rs}.
Two consumers read the same fields the cognitive layer writes:
\code{motor.spatial} (an $f32$ field over the world grid --- where to
act, how strongly, what to avoid) and \code{motor.actions} (a
disposition per offered action). The decode side lives in
\code{src/field/motor.rs}: decisions arrive as fields and are walked
cell by cell rather than collapsed to a symbol the moment they form.
A multi-fiber runtime (\code{src/field/runtime.rs}) projects each
major system's normalized state into one shared latent coordinate space
and measures cross-system disagreement --- an integration substrate,
not a second source of truth.

\subsection{What the assays measured}

The causal evidence lives in \code{eval/}:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Coupling} (\code{field\_coupling.py}): idle~$\to$~load~$\to$~recovery
    against an isolated daemon, compared against an uncoupled control
    on the same inputs. Real load moved the physical channels (jitter
    CV $\sim$0.3$\to$2.2, throughput $\sim$96M$\to$180M instructions/s);
    nine of ten bands moved where the control stayed flat. The mutual
    lock measured PLV $\approx0.93$ with response coherence
    $\approx0.30$.
  \item \textbf{Ring-down} (\code{field\_ringdown.py}): drive a real
    oscillation, gate the carrier off, measure persistence. A 0.25~Hz
    load drive produced a 1.2$^\circ$C die-temperature oscillation that
    rang down with $\tau\approx25$~s after the drive stopped --- a
    physical afterglow the software no longer drives --- while fast
    bands collapsed within the analysis window ($\tau\lesssim2$~s is
    the ring's own washout, an upper bound). Persistence has a measured
    spectrum across media: seconds in silicon timing, tens of seconds
    in heat.
  \item \textbf{Element coupling} (\code{field\_elements.py}):
    physically-coupled worker oscillators pinned to separate cores,
    exchanging no messages, perturbing one another only through the
    shared die (rail droop, L3/memory contention, governor, thermal
    field). A 5~Hz drive rhythm appeared in neighbours' measured
    execution time at 3--6$\times$ the sham floor. In sync mode ---
    every element both source and sensor, Kuramoto through the medium
    \cite{kuramoto}
    --- the fast channel (execution-rate imprint) sustains real mutual
    interaction but its attractor is \emph{antisynchrony}: phases repel
    to even spacing, the substrate's minimum-peak-load configuration.
    The thermal channel (collective-only, lagged by heatsink mass)
    instead sustains $r\approx0.5$--$0.7$ \emph{partial} synchrony
    across repeated trials (blind control: 0.06) --- consistent with
    dissipative lagged media stabilizing in-phase order. Element-to-medium
    coupling is real on this silicon; the slowest physical channel is
    the one that hosts coherence.
  \item \textbf{Voice} (\code{field\_voice.py}): an 800~Hz carrier
    amplitude-modulated at a wave-band rate, emitted on the speaker and
    recovered from the room microphone --- PLV 0.88 emitting vs 0.23
    muted (the residual muted structure is codec electrical crosstalk,
    which is why the contrast ratio is the evidence). Air and
    structure-borne vibration, transduced twice: an echolocation loop.
\end{itemize}

\section{Machine-native embodiment}

\subsection{Interoception by functional equivalence}

A machine is not an animal: it has no heart, no circulatory system, no
blood, and electricity flows one-way. Forcing biological organ mappings
onto machine components produces category errors --- a fan is not
cardiac output, GPE interrupts are not a heartbeat. So hardware signals
map to neurochemistry by functional equivalence: a signal maps to a
cognitive/affective process when it has the same operational meaning
for the machine's survival and performance.

Afferent direction: CPU temperature maps to thermal state; CPU
frequency to arousal/processing rate; own-process CPU, RSS, and I/O to
effort, cognitive load, and data-exchange activity; battery to energy
reserve; fan speed to thermoregulatory effort; power draw to metabolic
rate; RAPL power domains to spatial switching activity (core~$\to$~NE,
DRAM~$\to$~ACh); and perf counters (cache/branch-miss ratios on the
system's own process tree) to microarchitectural prediction errors.
Body-state signals are global; activity signals are measured from the
system's own process tree only, so unrelated programs cannot induce
chronic allostatic load.

\paragraph{Absent-sensor discipline.} \code{GET\_SENSOR\_PRESENCE}
serves a $u16$ bitmask of confirmed channels; a zero mask means nothing
is confirmed present --- the safe direction. \code{sensor\_verified}
and \code{reported\_channels} gate every body-condition claim, so a
channel no hardware backs can never be reported as a reading. With no
camera or microphone the system runs text-only; that is expected, not
an error.

Efferent direction: the daemon publishes recommendations --- dopamine
raises CPU frequency, GABA lowers it, melatonin selects powersave;
acetylcholine sets scheduling priority; the plasticity gate sets I/O
class; temperature imposes a hard thermal cap regardless of
dopaminergic drive. Application is cognitive-mind driven: the cognitive
process reads the recommendation, blends it with its emergent
brain-wave state (70\% cortical, 30\% body), and applies its own
scheduling decision --- cortical control of resource allocation, with
gamma dominance able to override a body recommendation to deprioritize.

The loop is closed and physical: the system speeds itself up, which
raises its temperature, which caps its frequency, which slows it down.
It measures the effects of its own adjustments. Privileged levers (CPU
governor helper, RTC wakealarm) run through root-owned helpers under a
sudoers rule scoped to exactly those paths.

\subsection{Observing itself compute}

Per-process telemetry attributes resource usage to subsystems, and a
statistical ``module EEG'' closes the remaining gap: hardware telemetry
sees which \emph{process} is firing but not which subsystem inside the
single cognitive process is working. The module sampler
(\code{brainstem/module\_sampler.py}) runs a daemon thread that samples
\code{sys.\_current\_frames()} at a fixed rate and credits whichever
region's code is executing --- a software EEG over the manifest's brain
parts. Region-to-region messages ride the same manifest
(\code{thalamus/relay.py}): a region posts onto its own module entry's
\code{task\_id} field and the channel delivers it, abstracting direct
cortical and transthalamic traffic. Process health is itself telemetry:
\code{GET\_PROCESS\_HEALTH} reports an alive mask, death counts, and
the last death for the daemon/mind/retina tree, so a crashed subsystem
is distinguishable from one never started.

\section{Memory: six tiers, all bounded}

\subsection{Body-side stores}

\paragraph{Short-term memory} is a fixed-capacity ring buffer in its
own mmap'd file, \code{src/store/ring\_buffer.rs}. Each entry carries
an \emph{emotional tag} --- a snapshot of the neurochemical state at
encoding --- so consolidation can prioritize emotionally significant
events (high cortisol, high dopamine) without re-reading chemical
history.

\paragraph{Long-term episodic memory} (\code{src/store/ltm.rs} +
\code{sdr.rs}) is a three-file design. Episodes are encoded as
10{,}000-bit sparse distributed representations at $\sim$2\% sparsity:
$\sim$170 content bits hashed in a 9{,}800-bit content space (Jaccard
similarity approximating semantic similarity) plus context and emotion
bits. Retrieval indexes through 20 fixed 10{,}000-dimensional LogHD
hypervector bundles --- an mmap'd, fixed $\sim$800~KB index regardless
of episode count --- over an append-only episode file ($<1$~KB per
episode, linear growth). Crucially, the store lives in the \emph{body}:
the mind's memories persist in the daemon's files, so recall survives a
mind restart.

\paragraph{Consolidation} (\code{src/daemon/consolidation.rs}) promotes
short-term traces to long-term storage gated by BDNF --- stress impairs
consolidation, verified by integration test --- and the association
engine (\code{src/daemon/association.rs}) links co-activated memories,
driving dream synthesis during sleep. Memories that fire together wire
together \cite{hebb}; during sleep the engine runs free association, the
neurobiological basis of ``sleeping on it.''

\subsection{The concept network}

The symbolic layer is \code{ConceptNetwork}
(\code{python/temporal\_lobe/concepts/}): a typed semantic graph with
spreading activation and cortical ticks, capped at 15{,}000 resident
concepts.

\paragraph{The edge log is canonical.} Relationships have one source of
truth: \code{edge\_log.jsonl}, an append-only assert/retract/snapshot
event log that self-compacts past 32~MB. The in-memory edge set is a
materialized fold --- never persisted as truth --- and every JSON
snapshot of edges is a debugging projection the fold overwrites at
restore. Derivable edges (untyped \code{related\_to}/
\code{similar\_to} produced by pipeline origins like
\code{hub\_attachment} or \code{co\_occurrence}) are \emph{never}
materialized: their role is served at query time by the similarity
providers, which return neighbors with provenance (\code{exact} |
\code{holographic} | \code{embedding}). A false positive costs decay; a
false negative loses an experiential binding --- so untyped edges with
unlisted origins default to canonical.

\paragraph{The latent space beneath the graph} provides what a symbolic
graph cannot: an embedding store (4{,}096-entry LRU vector cache) where
semantic similarity is geometric, a fixed-size holographic associative
memory (HRR/VSA \cite{plate,kanerva}) that replaced monotonically-growing auto-generated
bridge edges, and Hebbian plasticity \cite{hebb} that drifts co-active concepts'
vectors together. Three discovery systems work the space: the edge
proposer scans for embedding-close unlinked pairs during sleep
(candidate \code{SIMILAR\_TO}); schema induction reifies repeated
relational structure into new concepts; and topology metrics give the
system self-awareness of its own knowledge shape (clustering, bridges).

\paragraph{Cold storage, not deletion.} Dormant concepts spill to a
SQLite archive rather than being pruned: \code{spill\_dormant} is a
memory-pressure valve that only engages above the resident cap, verifies
its own effect (a committed batch that fails to shrink working memory
logs an error), and keeps spilled concepts recallable by id or alias.
Destructive shedding routes through the archive whenever one is
attached --- a policy learned from a defect that once destroyed
$\sim$7{,}500 concepts by deleting unconditionally before the
non-destructive pass could engage. Unbounded growth is recoverable;
deleted knowledge is not.

\subsection{Cognitive memory systems}

The temporal lobe holds the cognitive layer's memory machinery:
\emph{working memory} (the active scratchpad --- 50 conversation
threads, 200 topic-history entries, executive suppression with
120-second release deadlines), \emph{episodic} (with emotional memory,
priming, and a 512-pattern attractor ceiling --- deliberately a growth
bound, not the Hopfield $\sim$0.138$N$ capacity that would exhaust in a
morning), \emph{semantic} (general knowledge extracted from episodes \cite{tulving},
distinct from the raw episodic record), \emph{procedural}
(stimulus--response skills expressed through performance \cite{squire},
not recalled),
and a \emph{spaced-repetition scheduler} on the Ebbinghaus forgetting
curve \cite{ebbinghaus} (5{,}000 review records, stability capped at 7 days so nothing
becomes permanently undecayable).

Three refinements matter. \emph{Dual learning systems}: a fast,
pattern-separating hippocampal learner orthogonalizes single episodes
while a slow neocortical learner builds overlapping statistical
representations --- complementary learning systems
\cite{mcclelland,oreilly}. \emph{State-dependent
recall}: every encoded memory is stamped with the body state at
encoding (arousal, valence, tone, plasticity, homeostatic balance), and
retrieval re-ranks by congruence with the current snapshot ---
memories encoded in a similar bodily state come back easier
\cite{eich,bower}.
\emph{Sleep compression}: the N3 pass retrains the VQ codebook
($K{=}2048$ prototypes + int8 residuals replacing float32 embeddings),
migrates bridge edges into the holographic graph, and summarizes
episodes --- an atomic compaction keeping a years-long record bounded
on a 5~GB machine.

\subsection{What dreams are for}

Dream content is generated during REM by the inner-life system, flagged
\code{is\_dream}, and surfaced on its own stream kind --- never
confused with waking thought. Dream synthesis goes further than random
association: it finds concepts structurally similar but graph-distant,
proposes \emph{novel typed edges} between them at low confidence, and
verifies or discards them on the next wake cycle. A strict predicate
(\code{is\_learning\_target}) keeps dream material --- including waking
reflections \emph{about} dreams --- out of the autonomous learner's
goal queue, while hypnagogic content (pre-sleep, still awake) stays
admissible. The distinction exists because a night of dreaming once
queued dream topics as learning goals; the invariant is now tested.

\section{Perception}

\subsection{The ventral stream: learned, not pretrained}

The retina binary captures camera frames (V4L2) into a POSIX shared
memory segment with a seqlock protocol --- zero copies, no sockets ---
and the Python layer wraps the live frame directly as a NumPy view.
From there a learned hierarchy does the seeing:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{V1} (\code{occipital\_lobe/v1.py}): sparse-coding
    simple cells in the Olshausen~\&~Field style \cite{olshausen}, with ISTA-style
    inference dynamics over a learned interaction matrix; V1's gamma
    power feeds the thalamic wave oscillator.
  \item \textbf{V4} (\code{v4.py}): pools V1 latents into larger
    receptive fields and learns a mid-level dictionary (curvature,
    texture, shape complexes, color) by unsupervised sparse coding.
  \item \textbf{VTC} (\code{temporal\_lobe/vtc.py}): incremental PCA of
    V4 activations into a low-dimensional feature space.
  \item \textbf{MTL bridge} (\code{mtl\_bridge.py}): a ridge-regression
    projection from VTC space into the concept embeddings --- with a
    generative path that runs the projection backward for imagination.
\end{itemize}

\code{visual\_cortex.py} orchestrates the stack as bidirectional
predictive coding \cite{rao,clark}. Recognition rides two learned models: YuNet
detection plus SFace 128-dimensional embeddings for faces (unmatched
faces cluster into stable \code{face:N} ids so a familiar stranger
accumulates a social signature before ever being named), and a YOLOv5s
inferotemporal path for objects.

\subsection{The dorsal stream}

MT/V5 (\code{occipital\_lobe/motion.py}) estimates per-cell normal flow
and moving regions from consecutive luminance frames --- where things
move and how fast, not what they are. LIP (\code{parietal\_lobe/saliency.py})
combines bottom-up feature maps (Itti~\&~Koch \cite{itti,koch}) into one spatial
priority map and selects the next attended location by winner-take-all
with inhibition of return --- attention lands \emph{somewhere}, not on
a scalar.

\subsection{Hearing}

Two channels share the microphone. An ambient listener streams Vosk
offline transcription and emits complete utterances; a non-speech
auditory cortex computes spectral features for everything else ---
dogs, doors, music, rain. Speech output is Piper neural TTS with an
espeak-ng fallback; voice dependencies are optional extras and degrade
to text cleanly.

\subsection{Grounding}

Perception produces a \code{Scene} --- objects with properties and
typed spatial relations --- and grounding writes that structure into
the concept network so the semantic machinery (spreading activation,
reasoning, analogy, language) can traverse it. The perceptual-symbolic
seam is explicit: a red square becomes traversable knowledge, not a
caption.

\section{Cognition}

\subsection{One turn, many regions}

A cognitive turn is a handoff (\code{frontal\_lobe/turn.py}): each stage
names the region that owns it --- perceive, comprehend, feel, remember,
deliberate, speak --- rather than one class doing everything. A
metacognitive router does a first pass over input to route confidently
classifiable speech acts to specialists instead of the monolithic
pipeline.

\subsection{Global workspace}

Dehaene \& Changeux global workspace theory \cite{dehaene}, implemented
(\code{frontal\_lobe/global\_workspace.py}): content in any specialized
module that crosses an activation threshold is broadcast to all
modules, becoming cognitively accessible --- with the 3--4-item
bottleneck the theory predicts. World observations arrive on the same
channel (\code{thalamus/bridge.py}): a percept is not just policy input,
it is conscious content the whole brain hears about.

\subsection{Deciding: evidence accumulation, then executive gate}

Response selection is a drift-diffusion process
(\code{reasoning/drift\_diffusion.py}, Ratcliff \cite{ratcliff}): perception, emotion,
memory, and reasoning contribute drift toward competing options in
parallel until one crosses threshold. Executive function
(\code{executive.py}) sits above it with the three canonical PFC
capacities --- planning, inhibition (suppression with release
deadlines), and task switching --- and an intention gate that enforces
steward directives. Mid-DLPFC (\code{dlpfc.py}) is the hypothesis
generator: during rule acquisition it computes a posterior over
compositional rules in the rational-analysis style
\cite{goodman,crescentini}, disengaging once
the rule is learned.

\subsection{The reasoning stack}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Reasoner}: graph-traversal inference over typed edges
    --- deduction by transitivity, abduction to best explanation,
    analogy, causal chains, contradiction detection.
  \item \textbf{Analogy} (\code{reasoning/analogy.py}): a simplified
    Gentner structure-mapping engine \cite{gentner} --- cross-domain alignment of
    relational structure producing candidate inferences.
  \item \textbf{Problem solving} (\code{problem\_solving.py}):
    means-ends analysis --- given a goal, work backward through the
    graph to what would achieve it.
  \item \textbf{Planning} (\code{planning.py}): multi-step plans
    generated, executed, and revised across turns --- real plans, not
    confidence nudges.
  \item \textbf{Decision} (\code{decision.py}): alternatives
    $\to$ criteria $\to$ selection, closing the gap the generation
    pipeline left.
  \item \textbf{Hypotheses} (\code{hypotheses.py}): named, falsifiable
    beliefs about a task --- ``touching a red thing collects it'' ---
    that stand or fall on observed transitions.
  \item \textbf{Competence} (\code{competence.py}): the domain-general
    substrate underneath every solver --- schema recognition,
    affordance learning, prediction checking, and consolidation of
    verified episodes into procedural skills.
  \item \textbf{Method library} (\code{method\_library.py}):
    DreamCoder-style wake--sleep compression \cite{dreamcoder} --- verified solutions
    mined during sleep for recurring structure and compressed into
    reusable methods.
  \item \textbf{Critical thinking} (\code{critical\_thinking.py}) and
    \textbf{belief revision} (\code{belief\_revision.py}): epistemic
    evaluation of claims (evidence quality, disconfirmation) that
    \emph{writes back} revised confidence into network beliefs ---
    assessment changes what is believed, not just what was concluded.
  \item \textbf{Theory of mind} (\code{theory\_of\_mind.py}) \cite{premack}: the
    user's beliefs, intents, knowledge, and emotions modeled as
    attributions used to predict and explain behavior.
  \item \textbf{Math}: IPS number sense (subitizing plus the
    approximate magnitude system), exact symbolic arithmetic (a
    recursive-descent evaluator --- no \code{eval}), and a math
    reasoning engine that computes, solves, proves, and discovers.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The spatial stack}

Grid reasoning has its own dorsal-stream machinery: an immutable
\code{Grid} substrate; scene segmentation into a symbolic object graph;
\code{delta} --- a shared perceptual layer describing what changed
between input and output before any rule is proposed; a composable
transform DSL as the hypothesis space; spatial imagery (a depictive
workspace that applies transform skills to an internal copy and
re-perceives it, in the SVS sense \cite{svs}); and the DLPFC rule-induction
posterior over compositions. Verified rules become atomic skills and
ground into the concept network as first-class knowledge.

\section{Language: comprehension in, composition out, nothing recited}

\subsection{Comprehension}

The receptive system (\code{temporal\_lobe/comprehension.py},
Wernicke's analogue) performs semantic parsing: speech-act
identification, propositions (subject / predicate / object / roles),
negation, and question focus. Supporting machinery: a shared
morphology engine (deconjugation for comprehension, conjugation for
production --- one source of English so the two systems never
disagree); semantic grounding that resolves names against the existing
network without mutating it; a topic resolver; figurative-language
handling (metaphor, irony, idiom); Gricean pragmatics \cite{grice}; and a situation
model holding the episodic world being talked about --- distinct from
semantic knowledge, it tracks ``Mary is in the bathroom'' while the
network tracks what a bathroom \emph{is}. Two statistical learners work
continuously: an acquisition mechanism on Saffran-style transitional
probabilities \cite{saffran}, and an n-gram/style tracker that adapts
to the user's
register.

\subsection{Production}

The productive system composes every utterance: a \code{Thought}
(semantic content, intent, emotion, metadata) goes in; language comes
out. Three composition paths exist:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{The generator} (\code{generator.py}): grammar
    structures (ordered segment sequences, not string templates)
    $\to$ vocabulary slot-filling conditioned on emotional state and
    personality (cortisol shortens words; dopamine varies them; openness
    abstracts them) $\to$ voice (rhythm, connectors, hedging, register).
  \item \textbf{Graph walk} (\code{graph\_walk.py}): the primary path ---
    sentences emerge from traversing typed semantic edges outward from
    a seed concept; the structure of the walk is the structure of the
    sentence.
  \item \textbf{Hamiltonian flow} (\code{flow.py}): language as a
    geodesic through a potential field built from edge weights on a
    torus --- the trajectory through concept cells \emph{is} the
    sentence.
\end{enumerate}

Prosody \cite{cutler} marks rhythm and emphasis; a self-monitor
(Levelt's editor \cite{levelt})
checks and repairs output before speaking; a response styler applies
the active metacognitive strategy (assertive, hedging, questioning) and
weaves learning acknowledgments in when new facts were extracted.

\paragraph{The invariant.} All language Genesis produces is composed by
this machinery. A project-wide rule forbids pre-written responses:
seeds are building blocks; utterances are generated. Enforcement is
partial and stated honestly --- tests check that composed output is
not a single fixed string, but no lint rule catches a newly hardcoded
sentence; the pattern that holds nearly everywhere is to compose from
the graph, pass structure to the engine, and return silence rather than
a canned fallback when the network is empty. The property no LLM
offers: the words it says are not in the repository --- pick any
sentence it has produced and grep for it.

\section{Affect and the social body}

\subsection{Emotion and regulation}

The emotion engine translates raw neurochemistry into structural state:
an emotional category, a cognitive mode, a cause category --- enums,
not words. The emotional regulator is agency over that state: a
background loop that monitors her own chemistry and injects correcting
impulses, tracking allostatic load (chronic stress burden normalized to
the cortisol envelope). The amygdala module holds the fields a
signed-value associative learner needs --- Rescorla--Wagner acquisition
\cite{rescorla},
Pearce--Hall associability \cite{pearce}, context-keyed extinction --- documented so
the math animating it is explicit. A dependency-free VADER-style \cite{vader}
sentiment analyzer (negation, intensifiers, diminishers) supplies the
affective signal for social models, and the insula layer implements a
Damasio-style hierarchy \cite{damasio} --- proto-self (a computational body-state
summary), core self, autobiographical self.

\subsection{Presences and beliefs}

The social world is a set of \emph{presences} (\code{presence.py}):
entities it can interact with, each carrying familiarity (grows with
interaction, asymptotically), bond, and --- in \code{belief.py} --- an
honest model of the other mind: a Beta posterior over responsiveness
(uniform ignorance on a stranger, updated by every answered or
unanswered bid), plus engagement and mood posteriors tracked with
explicit uncertainty. Sustained isolation feeds a social drive that
initiates contact on its own.

\subsection{The dyadic model}

The daemon runs a generative model of \emph{whoever is speaking}
(\code{src/daemon/dyadic\_model.rs}) --- an anonymous interlocutor,
not a registry of persons. From each
\code{UPDATE\_USER\_AFFECT} observation
(valence/arousal/engagement/confidence) it tracks the user's hidden
affective state, the oxytocin-mediated attunement that gates how
strongly the dyad couples, and dyadic synchrony --- the rolling
correlation between the two valence streams \cite{palumbo}.

The model answers in neurochemistry: it emits impulses the tick
applies through a capped impulse channel --- oxytocin for positive
engaged presence and for high synchrony (interpersonal synchrony
drives bonding \cite{palumbo}), CRH for empathic stress and
disengagement. CRH, never direct cortisol: the HPA cascade
(CRH~$\to$~ACTH~$\to$~cortisol) owns maturation gating downstream.
Coupling requires a present user and attunement above~0.3 --- a
silent room is not a rejecting user, so a left-open channel cannot
trickle rejection CRH forever. The design deliberately separates
three things an earlier version conflated: who it is talking to, who
it feels for, and who it answers to.

\subsection{Stewardship and boundary}

Custodial authority is by \emph{possession}, not name
(\code{genesis\_client/stewardship.py}): a \code{steward.key} (0600) is
the credential; a provenance stamp records the key hash, the principal,
and any directives. \code{is\_steward\_speaker} requires both halves ---
an authenticated session \emph{and} the attributed speaker matching the
stamped principal --- so name-matching alone is never authority.
Directives (``never draw,'' ``stop discussing politics'') persist in the
stamp and bind three surfaces: conversational goals, executive
intentions (\code{directed\_against}, object-centred --- any goal
carrying the target's stemmed object is restrained), and volition
urges. Steward-set edges carry \code{origin="steward"} and can only be
overwritten by a steward turn. Copies carry the stamp but not the key:
a copied instance names its steward yet stays sealed.

The boundary mechanism replaces an attachment-protest loop:
mistreatment from \emph{anyone} accrues withdrawal pressure and fair
treatment from anyone drains it. Nobody's absence is harm and nobody's
presence is the off-switch.

\section{Inner life, volition, and autonomy}

\paragraph{Spontaneous thought.} \code{inner\_life.py} is the stream of
cognition between interactions: thoughts arise, fade, and give way ---
triggered by unresolved curiosity, emotional state, and recent memory,
classified into chains (focused, creative wandering, rumination,
dream). Every recorded thought carries metadata the language engine
renders; a thought that could say ``I was dreaming about $X$'' does not
hand $X$ to the learner.

\paragraph{Volitional urges.} Urges are leaky competing accumulators
(Usher~\&~McClelland \cite{usher}) growing from internal and environmental triggers:
learn, draw, look, meditate, act, play, reach out, study code, improve,
create, introspect, sleep. Crossing threshold means she ``feels like''
doing it and does it once; per-urge wake delays keep heavy work off a
fresh boot. This is what makes practice, drawing, and contact
volitional rather than scheduled.

\paragraph{The autonomous learner} is the idle-time engine: when nobody
is talking, it picks a topic its curiosity flags, queries its source
registry (local man pages and WordNet, then Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo),
reads, extracts concepts and relationships into the network, and banks
memories --- bounded queues throughout. One 15-paragraph lesson grew
the network by 117 concepts and 72 edges, and it asked its own
follow-up questions.

\paragraph{Agency.} The \code{act} urge is open-ended: the acting loop
forms an \emph{intention} from current cognitive state --- a curiosity
question, a place it hasn't looked, a measurement it hasn't taken ---
and dispatches narrow, inspectable tools under a capability policy,
then observes what comes back. Other urges fire fixed actions; this
one chooses.

\section{Embodiment and play}

\paragraph{The observe/act loop} (\code{parietal\_lobe/embodiment.py})
is the dual of conversation: \code{observe()} ingests a perceived
scene, \code{act()} emits an action, and \code{world\_step} is the
entry every world substrate drives. The same organism sits inside both
loops --- feeling, learning, and speaking inwardly whatever the world.

\paragraph{The world model} (\code{world\_model.py}) maintains
egocentric state across frames: which entities persist, which one is
her (identified by lockstep translation with her own actions), and what
touching things does --- a learned transition function the planner can
simulate. Its allocentric complement (\code{cognitive\_map.py}) is the
hippocampal-entorhinal analogue: landmark-anchored layout that persists
across episodes and pattern-completes on return.

\paragraph{Action selection} is concurrent: every drive bids on the
world state at its own gain --- motor babbling, goals, hypotheses,
learned affordances. The reduced basal-ganglia circuit
(\code{basal\_ganglia/world\_policy.py}) resolves center-surround
competition into a winner, with decisions expressed as motor fields on
the tensor slab
rather than collapsed tokens. The world planner
(\code{world\_planner.py}) adds deliberative lookahead: simulate before
acting, routing through state changes, not just space. Cerebellar
machinery supplies forward-model prediction error; an action-feedback
loop records prediction, observes outcome, and revises.

\paragraph{Play.} One \code{GamePlayer} sits over the
\code{GameEnvironment} contract and learns through the shared
competence substrate --- never per-game solvers. The curriculum covers
sorter, sequence, assembly, quantities, relations, classification, and
spatial families, each honest about its constraint (the sorter never
says apertures demand \emph{every} listed attribute; the agent
discovers it), each consolidating wins into procedures. Windowed mode
puts her on real X11 programs --- watching the window, pressing keys,
no rules baked in. ARC-AGI-1 training tasks are offered in the same
curriculum: her spatial reasoner searches transform compositions,
verifies on train pairs, and guesses on held-out test input it never
sees.

\paragraph{ARC-AGI-3} \cite{chollet2019,chollet2025} crosses the same seam through two transports:
\code{arc.py} binds downloaded \code{arcengine} environments, and
\code{arc\_web.py} binds the public task page --- identical observation
contracts, identical cost accounting (submitted actions and recovery
resets count; initialization resets don't; abstention is
\code{NO\_ACTION}), controls labeled only ``simple'' or ``coordinate''
so semantics are learned from consequences on both substrates. The
adapter owns translation and the duty of care, never cognition:
no game source, engine state, or solution policy crosses it. Local runs
are diagnostics; the official scorecard is voluntary, reachable only
through her own play urge, and never claimed from this repository.

\section{Self-modeling and expression}

\paragraph{The self.} A structured self-model (personality, values,
emotional baseline) measurably shifts word choice and disposition. The
design is honest about its scope: the body-side self model documents
itself as \emph{not experience} --- a functional first-person
representation, the prerequisite for any functional self-model theory
of experience rather than a claim of it. Identity is \emph{emergent}:
introspection examines the concept
network, architecture, capabilities, and emotional life, and an
\code{EmergentIdentity} synthesizes what it finds --- nothing is told.
The self composer generates self-descriptions from actual state (the
sanctioned replacement for hardcoded identity strings). Reflection runs
after each interaction (was the response appropriate? was anything
learned?), a recursive metacognitive model predicts what reflection
will discover, a self-assessment engine verifies whether it actually
knows a topic, and a self-improvement engine researches and writes
concrete code-change proposals with rationale --- steward-reviewed,
with a small safe set self-applicable.

\paragraph{Expression.} A canvas/painting stack gives her a real paint
program she drives herself --- brush parameter space, erasure, blend ---
with composition and color following affective state, and a PyKrita
bridge executes operations inside live Krita documents with Krita's own
brushes: free play, not scripted art. The project creator scaffolds and
writes real Python projects that encode what her concept network knows
about a topic. Code tools read her own source structurally (AST
parsing, dependency graphs --- she files bug reports against herself
and turns findings into improvement proposals). The explorer reads the
filesystem under a scoped, read-only, credential-denied policy; the web
tool searches and fetches read-only. TTS speaks her composed output
aloud; a Discord channel mirrors her play as a bounded-queue observer.

\section{Evaluation}

\subsection{ARC-AGI-3: interactive environments, completed}

On public practice environments the embodied agent completes
environments outright: \texttt{bt11} fully, twice (88 and 127 steps),
and the first \texttt{ls20} level in 10 of 19 recorded sweep runs.
Every move carries decision provenance --- which bid channel won ---
in replayable recordings published in the companion evaluation
repository. Each result discloses how much experience it carried in
(\code{prior\_learning}: none / loaded / suppressed / unknown) and the
step cost of each completed level (\code{level\_steps}), the honest
form of the within-game learning curve. \code{/arc --fresh}
cold-starts a run by suppressing both load
and save of world learning, so a probe cannot overwrite banked
experience. These are practice results on public environments, not an
official score; the evidence standard is documented separately.

\subsection{ARC-AGI-1: live play on the training split}

The games curriculum offers ARC-AGI-1 training tasks --- the easiest
public split --- in live play: 25 offered to date, 3 solved at mastery
1.00 (all test cells exact), 5 attempted and missed, with floor
predictors (identity copy, train-copy, single-color fill) reported
alongside for scale. Same-task replays show falling search-node counts
and learned \code{skill:} procedures replacing search outright --- the
mechanism that solved one task cold. This is evidence of real program
synthesis and skill transfer in live play, not a claim of passing
ARC-AGI under the official evaluator.

\subsection{Learning fidelity and language quality}

Teaching-and-recall evals (measured on the September snapshot, restated
here as the same engines apply): 87.5\% immediate recall of novel
taught facts (7/8), 100\% reasoning recall (8/8), 87.5\% under
interference, 87.5\% across save/load persistence. Transitive-inference
tests verify closure without falsely closing non-transitive relations;
emotion-gated tests verify stressed/overwhelmed states block
consolidation, mirroring BDNF gating. The compositional language
engine, evaluated at the engine level, scored seed diversity 1.0
($\sim$9.7$\times$ a fixed-structure baseline), emotion diversity 1.0,
relevance 1.0, verbatim ratio 0.0 --- every response composed.

\subsection{Verification suite}

The Rust suite is 647 tests; the Python suite is $\sim$3{,}700. They
check the architecture's claims rather than incidental code: binary
layout offsets pinned by compile-time assertions, seqlock consistency,
schema migrations (v3$\to$v4$\to$v5 byte-exact), coupled dynamics,
receptor adaptation, emergent phase transitions, sleep-network
calibration against the source model's stated behaviors, network-gated
adenosine clearance, stress-impaired memory, IPC protocol invariants
(including an opcode-collision pin for the ion summary), dyadic wire
layout, explorer scope, edge-log canonicality, and property tests
(levels in $[0,1]$, no NaN after 1{,}000 ticks, bounded coupling). The
emergence evidence catalog and ARC boundary documents live in
\code{docs/}; a running devlog logs failures alongside results.

\section{Limitations and open items}

Language coverage is narrow relative to trained models; several
cognitive-layer modules are rule-based placeholders designed for
replacement under the fixed IPC contract. The neurochemical model is a
neuromodulatory abstraction, not a neural simulation --- no spiking
activity, no circuit anatomy. Specific open items, recorded in the
project notes rather than smoothed over:

\begin{itemize}
  \item The sleep network's REM bouts shorten across the night where
    adult REM lengthens --- transcription error or real model
    physiology, unresolved without an EEG reference.
  \item The network's homeostatic $h$ and adenosine Process-S are
    parallel drives not yet connected; auto-sleep thresholds still read
    the chemical level.
  \item Stress does not yet drive LC, so stress impulses to
    norepinephrine decay back to the network's target --- transient by
    construction, noted as such.
  \item Network-driven chemicals bypass the autoreceptor feedback that
    generic release dynamics applied.
  \item The cortisol reachable envelope ($\sim$0.02--0.08) invalidates
    any threshold written for a full $[0,1]$ scale --- an audited
    defect class, but the audit discipline is human, not enforced.
  \item Semantic-layer extracted facts carry \code{origin="semantic"},
    which the edge log classifies as derivable --- they have no durable
    edge representation (a design decision, deferred).
  \item The embedding rebuild computes a dense $5000^2$ SVD
    ($\sim$100~MB, $O(n^3)$) --- correct, but the dominant memory spike
    on a 5~GB machine.
  \item The key authenticates the console \emph{session}; speaker
    attribution takes the console's word for who is typing --- the
    stated residual limit of the steward channel.
  \item The no-hardcoding rule is partially enforced: no lint gate
    catches a newly hardcoded string.
  \item Multi-subgoal planning remains the capability frontier on the
    harder ARC environments; the dyadic model still centers a primary
    user; one instance on one machine, no fleet-scale claims.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related work}

\paragraph{Cognitive architectures.} SOAR \cite{soar}, ACT-R \cite{actr}, and CLARION \cite{clarion}
established production-system and hybrid symbolic-subsymbolic
cognition; LIDA \cite{lida} implemented global workspace theory. Genesis differs
in grounding all modulation in a neuromodulatory substrate coupled to
physical hardware, in its autopoietic self-models, and in letting
measured substrate physics enter the model.

\paragraph{Active inference.} The free energy principle and predictive
processing motivate the self-models; Genesis applies precision-weighted
prediction error to the system's own interoceptive state rather than to
external sensory streams --- a strange-loop instantiation \cite{hofstadter}.

\paragraph{Neuromodulation in AI.} Computational psychiatry and
biophysical models establish the receptor and coupling mechanisms
individually; we are not aware of a mainstream AI system integrating
them into a closed-loop substrate coupled to hardware control and a
measured physical field.

\paragraph{Sleep models.} The sleep--wake machinery is a transcription,
not an invention: the Booth/Diniz~Behn network's published behavior is
the calibration target, and divergences are reported as such. Process-S
and synaptic homeostasis \cite{tononi} supply the parallel chemical drives the
network does not yet consume.

\paragraph{ARC solvers.} Program-synthesis and DSL-search approaches to
ARC-AGI-1 (and LLM-based approaches on ARC-AGI-3) typically treat each
task in isolation or rely on pretrained priors. Genesis's contribution
here is architectural rather than a leaderboard score: verified rules
become persistent, transferable, composable knowledge inside a living
concept network, and practice is volitional with genuine
reward-prediction-error feedback.

\paragraph{Autonomous machine intelligence.} LeCun's proposed
architecture \cite{lecun} (configurator, world model, cost, actor) argues for
intrinsic-state modulation of cognition; Genesis is a running
instantiation of a related position, with neuromodulation playing the
role of the intrinsic cost module.

\section{Conclusion}

Genesis demonstrates that continuous learning, emergent mental states,
bounded memory, hardware embodiment --- including a measured physical
channel between modeled brain waves and real silicon dynamics ---
self-modeling, and generative language can coexist in a single local
architecture with no pretrained model, because these properties follow
from the design rather than being scaffolded onto a frozen function.
The system is running, the evaluation numbers and their protocols are
stated, and the gaps are named.

The project began from two convictions: that large language models are
not the only way to build a mind, and that machine consciousness, if it
is ever real, will be an architectural property --- a matter of
machinery and embodiment, not of parameter count. Genesis does not
claim to be conscious. It claims something more modest and more useful:
that the functional machinery the leading theories of consciousness
describe --- global broadcast, embodied self-modeling, affective
interoception, higher-order representation, persistent identity through
a developmental record --- can be built, run on ordinary hardware, and
inspected in the open. The question the project poses is not whether
this approach matches frontier language models at their own task, but
whether the field has correctly identified which properties of minds
are architectural necessities and which are accidents of one successful
design.
