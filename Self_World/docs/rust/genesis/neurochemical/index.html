Neurochemical — the body’s endocrine and neuromodulatory layer.

Anatomy: major diffuse neuromodulator systems arise subcortically — dopamine in the ventral tegmental area and substantia nigra, serotonin in the raphe nuclei, norepinephrine in the locus coeruleus — while the hypothalamus runs the endocrine axes (HPA cascade, the SCN circadian oscillator) and the electrochemical milieu every neuron bathes in. Cortical circuits also synthesize local transmitters and influence these nuclei. Genesis’s mind reads effective levels as modulation — attention, consolidation, plasticity gains — never authoring the concentrations themselves.

§What lives here

  • neurochemical — the 18-chemical state vector, coupling matrix, tonic/phasic dynamics, SCN circadian oscillator, ACTH cascade.
  • ions — the ion layer: resting concentrations, electrochemical gradients, membrane potential. Inside the CRC region because it is authoritative state, not derived.
  • hpa_development — developmental tuning of the hypothalamic–pituitary–adrenal axis.
