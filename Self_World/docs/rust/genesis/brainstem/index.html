Brainstem — the autonomic core of the body.

Anatomy: arousal and visceral reflexes, interacting with hypothalamic homeostasis. It also houses the monoaminergic nuclei that broadcast neuromodulators upward (locus coeruleus → norepinephrine, raphe → serotonin, VTA/SN → dopamine), and the reticular activating system that sets arousal zones. In Genesis the daemon’s own clock and body-maintenance loop is this region: it ticks whether or not the cognitive mind is alive.

§What lives here (implementation stays in crate::daemon /

crate::state; this region is the anatomical view)

  • tick — the pacemaker. Sole authority on time and physics: integrates neurochemistry, circadian phase, and sleep staging at TICK_INTERVAL_MS, lease-driven by the mind while it lives.
  • interoception — visceral sensing of the machine body: CPU, thermal, battery, disk. The body’s read on its own condition.
  • cpufreq — autonomic frequency/thermal policy (the brainstem’s homeostatic reflexes, applied to silicon).
  • active_inference — the body’s anticipatory regulation: free energy, precision, surprise, allostatic load — published as inference signals the mind observes rather than drives.
  • zones — arousal/task zone state (the reticular activating system’s projection target).
  • core_state, header, sanitize, legacy_v3 — the somatic ground truth: the fixed-layout body state every region shares, its integrity checks, and the frozen prior schema.
