First-order metabolic leak for CRH, per second.

Measured plasma CRH clearance is biexponential: human CRH disappears with t½ ≈ 30.5 min (J Endocrinol 1992, 133:487), and ovine CRH in humans with fast/slow components of ~11.6 and ~73 min (JCEM 1984, 58:192; components ~6 and ~55 min in JCEM 1982). The modeled variable is the hypothalamic stress-drive signal — the hypophyseal-portal CRH pulse rather than a peripheral plasma measurement — so the fast component is used: 1.9e-3/s is a ~6 min half-life, long enough for the signal to outlast a brief impulse and short enough to clear once its cause is gone.
