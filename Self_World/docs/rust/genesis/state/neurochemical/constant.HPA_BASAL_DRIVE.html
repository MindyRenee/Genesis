Basal HPA-axis ACTH drive at the circadian peak (multiplied by hpa_circadian_factor, scaled by maturation).

Without a basal term the axis produces cortisol only under CRH (stress) drive, and metabolic clearance pulls the level to a literal 0.0 — an absent hormone, not a resting one. Basal drive holds the nadir near the measured resting floor (~0.02) and the circadian peak below the stress gate (0.06): tonic presence, not distress.

Sizing: the cortisol equilibrium solves acth·0.01/s = C·(1.75e-4 + 0.8·C) — the nonlinear clearance composes at ~0.8·excess per second at the 10 Hz reference rate. Drive 0.05 gives C ≈ 0.025 at the peak factor and ~0.011 at nadir — inside the measured envelope (resting 0.02, peak 0.08), and maturation scales the whole axis for an immature body.

(Weitzman et al., 1971; Scheer et al., 2009 — the SCN-driven circadian rhythm dominates the modest suppression from sleep.)
