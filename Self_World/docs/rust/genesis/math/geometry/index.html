Geometry — the plane, computed.

Points are plain pairs of f64. The machine’s toolkit: distances and orientations, segment intersections, the shoelace area, Andrew’s monotone-chain convex hull, triangle centers, and ray-cast containment. Exact where exactness is possible, optional where the math can fail.

orient
Orientation: the signed cross product (b-a) x (c-a). Positive = counterclockwise, negative = clockwise, zero = collinear. This one number decides most planar questions.
