Complex analysis — the plane where every root exists.

Complex is a pair of f64s; the operations are the field’s own: arithmetic, conjugate, modulus, argument, exp/log/pow through Euler, roots of unity, polynomial evaluation by Horner, and the Möbius map. The imaginary unit needs no name here — only arithmetic.
