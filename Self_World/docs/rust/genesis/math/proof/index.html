Module proof

Expand description

Proof — the third pillar of mathematical thinking.

The machine’s role in proof is the one that is decidable: checking. A formula is a tree of atoms joined by connectives; a sequent Γ ⊢ φ asks whether φ holds under every assignment that makes Γ true — and with finitely many atoms the answer is a truth table, not a guess. The inference rules below are the classical kernel — each takes premises and a claimed conclusion and says whether the step is licensed.

Induction is handled honestly: the machine can verify a base case and the step at every integer in a bounded range — that is evidence, never a proof, and the return type says so.

and_elim
Conjunction elimination: from A ∧ B conclude A (or B).
and_intro
Conjunction introduction: from A and B, conclude A ∧ B.
by_cases
Case analysis: if premises ⊢ A∨B, premises∪{A} ⊢ C and premises∪{B} ⊢ C, then premises ⊢ C. Exhaustion of cases.
by_contradiction
Proof by contradiction: premises ∪ {¬φ} ⊢ ⊥ iff premises ⊢ φ. The sequent form of reductio — if assuming the opposite is unsatisfiable, the claim follows. Both sides are checked, and they agree only when the meta-theorem holds here.
de_morgan
De Morgan’s law as a rewrite: ¬(A ∧ B) ⇄ (¬A ∨ ¬B), checked in both directions.
deduction
Deduction theorem (implication introduction): if premises ∪ {A} ⊢ B, then premises ⊢ A → B. Checked semantically — the meta-step that turns an assumption into an implication.
entails
Sequent check Γ ⊢ φ: under every assignment where all premises hold, does the conclusion hold? The semantic turnstile — entailment, decided by the table.
hypothetical_syllogism
Hypothetical syllogism: from (A → B) and (B → C), conclude (A → C).
induction_evidence
Bounded induction evidence: verify base(n0) and every step k → k+1 across [lo, hi] under predicate pred. Returns the checked range on success — EVIDENCE, never a proof. The quantifier over all naturals stays beyond the machine’s reach, and this signature refuses to pretend otherwise.
is_contradiction
Is the formula true under no assignment — a contradiction?
is_tautology
Is the formula true under every assignment — a tautology? Decidable: 2^n assignments, checked exhaustively.
modus_ponens
Modus ponens: from (A → B) and A, conclude B.
modus_tollens
Modus tollens: from (A → B) and ¬B, conclude ¬A.
or_intro
Disjunction introduction: from A conclude A ∨ B for any B.
