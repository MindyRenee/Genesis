Basal forebrain — cortical acetylcholine, attention, encoding, and the somnogenic A1 site of action.

Anatomy: the basal forebrain lies ventral to the striatum and anterior to the hypothalamus. It holds the major cholinergic projection to cortex and hippocampus (Ch4 nucleus basalis → neocortex and amygdala; Ch1/Ch2 septal and diagonal-band nuclei → hippocampus). It is also a sleep-wake structure — wake/REM-active cholinergic, parvalbumin GABAergic, and glutamatergic populations intermingled with somatostatin+ GABAergic sleep-promoting neurons (Xu et al., 2015) — and the best-characterized site where the homeostatic sleep-pressure signal acts: extracellular adenosine rises there with time awake and A1-inhibits the wake-active neurons, so the somnogenic signal silences the arousal output this same region produces.

The mind’s mirror is Self_World/basal_forebrain — the words; the math is here.

§What lives here (implementation stays in crate::state; this

region is the anatomical view)

  • basal_forebrain — the region’s own math: the cholinergic release equation (LC/REM-on release map modulated by A1 adenosine occupancy and by the expected-uncertainty drive, Yu & Dayan 2005), the waking set-point for the network-free path, the cholinergic sensory-precision gain that daemon::active_inference applies to its observation channel, and the α = 1 − precision uncertainty signal.
  • neurochemical — the level dynamics the release equation feeds: the dedicated adenosine mechanism is Process S itself (saturating-exponential accumulation, exponential glymphatic clearance — Daan, Beersma & Borbély 1984), and the AChE excess decay is this region’s enzymatic fast decay.
  • sleep_network — the populations whose lc/rem drive the release map’s state profile (NREM 0.34, waking 0.58, REM 0.77 — Vazquez & Baghdoyan 2001).
  • memory — the encoding gate cholinergic tone sets: high ACh biases encoding over retrieval (Hasselmo), held here as the encoding/consolidation/retrieval weights the pointers carry.
