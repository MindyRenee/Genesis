1//! Developmental competence of the HPA axis.
2//!
3//! ## Why this is a curve and not a ramp
4//!
5//! The stress-hyporesponsive period (SHRP) is real and worth modelling: a
6//! neonatal window in which the adrenocortical axis fails to mount a
7//! glucocorticoid response to stress, protecting the developing brain
8//! from cortisol's neurotoxic effects on synaptogenesis. In rodents it
9//! runs from postnatal day 4 to 14 (Schmidt et al., 2003; Levine,
10//! 2005). In humans the equivalent is thought to fall between roughly
11//! 6 and 12 months, possibly to 15 months, and its precise end depends
12//! on the quality of care the infant receives — poor care ends it
13//! earlier (Gunnar & Donzella, 2002; Gunnar & Cheatham, 2003).
14//!
15//! The thing this must not become is a permanent ramp. The previous
16//! implementation computed competence as `ltm_episode_count / 10_000`,
17//! which meant cortisol was exactly zero until 10,000 lifetime
18//! episodes and the axis was only fully online in the system's final
19//! developmental phase. Three things are wrong with that:
20//!
21//!   1. **It is not a biological quantity.** The SHRP is defined in
22//!      postnatal days, not episodic memory. A system that stores
23//!      memories does not thereby have a stress axis, and one that
24//!      stores many does not thereby become adult in a biologically
25//!      meaningful way.
26//!
27//!   2. **It disabled the primary mediator of allostatic load.**
28//!      McEwen's construct names cortisol, epinephrine and
29//!      norepinephrine as the primary mediators. If cortisol cannot
30//!      exist for 10,000 episodes, then a measure built on the stress
31//!      axis cannot see stress — and the system is blind precisely
32//!      where the framework says the cost is greatest, because early
33//!      experience is when a developing mind is most vulnerable.
34//!
35//!   3. **The real trajectory is not monotone.** After the SHRP the
36//!      axis over-responds. Across the peripubertal period HPA
37//!      responses are "exaggerated relative to adults, both in terms
38//!      of ACTH and glucocorticoid release" (PMC4867107), with a
39//!      documented lag in negative feedback — the adolescent animal
40//!      takes longer both to mount and to recover a response. A
41//!      single ramp from 0 to 1 cannot represent that shape.
42//!
43//! So the developmental curve here is hyporesponsive early, crosses
44//! into full adult competence, and passes through a transient
45//! amplification before settling. That is the shape the literature
46//! actually describes, and it is expressed against elapsed lifetime
47//! (the clock the SHRP is defined in) rather than against a count of
48//! stored episodes.
49
50/// Experience at which development is complete.
51///
52/// Developmental progress is driven by accumulated experience rather
53/// than by wall-clock time. The reason is that the two clocks are not
54/// commensurable: the stress-hyporesponsive period is bounded by a
55/// year of an infant's life and adolescence by puberty, but a
56/// computational mind's development is a function of what it has
57/// processed. Mapping years onto a machine's uptime would either
58/// immobilise the axis for most of the system's life or make
59/// development instantaneous, and neither is a defensible reading of
60/// the phenomenon.
61///
62/// Elapsed time is retained only as a floor-advancer, for a system
63/// that has existed a long time without accumulating episodes. It can
64/// carry development forward; it can never hold it back.
65const ADULT_EPISODE_COUNT: f32 = 1_000.0;
66
67/// Wall-clock span over which elapsed time alone can carry
68/// development forward, in seconds (~2 years).
69///
70/// Only reached by a system that has lived this long without
71/// accumulating episodes, which is the case this exists to handle.
72const DEVELOPMENT_SPAN_SECS: f32 = 63_072_000.0;
73
74/// Fraction of development spent in the SHRP.
75///
76/// The period is a small fraction of a life — on the order of a year
77/// against decades — and modelled here as the first ~5% of
78/// development, so it is genuinely brief rather than a long stretch of
79/// a machine's life in which the stress axis is switched off.
80const SHRP_FRACTION: f32 = 0.02;
81
82/// Centre of the peripubertal amplification, as a fraction of
83/// developmental progress.
84const PERIPUBERTAL_CENTRE: f32 = 0.62;
85
86/// Half-width of the peripubertal amplification.
87const PERIPUBERTAL_WIDTH: f32 = 0.14;
88
89/// Peak amplification of the axis during the peripubertal window.
90///
91/// Documented as responses being "exaggerated relative to adults" in
92/// both ACTH and glucocorticoid release, with a lag in negative
93/// feedback that lengthens both the rise and the recovery. 1.25 keeps
94/// that qualitative feature without letting the axis run away.
95const PERIPUBERTAL_GAIN: f32 = 1.25;
96
97/// Upper bound on competence. Above 1.0 only during the peripubertal
98/// window, where the axis is documented to over-respond; a bound is
99/// kept so a corrupted input cannot drive ACTH arbitrarily high.
100const MAX_COMPETENCE: f32 = 1.5;
101
102/// Competence of the HPA axis at a given developmental point.
103///
104/// Normally in [0, 1], where 1.0 is fully adult. It exceeds 1.0 only
105/// inside the peripubertal window, where the axis is documented to
106/// respond more strongly than it does in adulthood.
107///
108/// `elapsed_secs` is wall-clock time since the state was created and
109/// `ltm_count` is accumulated episodic experience; development
110/// advances on whichever is further along, and can be carried by
111/// either. This is a multiplier on ACTH production and on the
112/// CRH→ACTH drive, so 1.0 means fully adult.
113pub fn hpa_competence(elapsed_secs: f32, ltm_count: u64) -> f32 {
114    let by_experience = (ltm_count as f32 / ADULT_EPISODE_COUNT).clamp(0.0, 1.0);
115    let by_elapsed = (elapsed_secs / DEVELOPMENT_SPAN_SECS).clamp(0.0, 1.0);
116    let progress = by_experience.max(by_elapsed);
117
118    // The SHRP. Competence is near zero across a brief early window
119    // and climbs after it, as a smoothstep rather than a cutoff —
120    // real adrenal sensitivity recovers gradually, and a
121    // discontinuity would let one tick switch the whole axis on or off.
122    let mature = smoothstep(SHRP_FRACTION, SHRP_FRACTION * 3.0, progress);
123
124    // The peripubertal window: a transient amplification riding on top
125    // of full competence, decaying back to 1.0. The axis over-responds
126    // during adolescence, so competence there is modestly above adult.
127    let t = (progress - PERIPUBERTAL_CENTRE) / PERIPUBERTAL_WIDTH;
128    let bump = (-t * t).exp();
129    // Note the upper bound is above 1.0, not at it. An earlier
130    // version clamped to 1.0, which silently erased the peripubertal
131    // amplification entirely — the raw value there is 1.25, and
132    // clamping made adolescence indistinguishable from adulthood.
133    (mature * (1.0 + (PERIPUBERTAL_GAIN - 1.0) * bump)).clamp(0.0, MAX_COMPETENCE)
134}
135
136/// Smoothstep from `edge0` to `edge1`, clamped outside.
137fn smoothstep(edge0: f32, edge1: f32, x: f32) -> f32 {
138    if (edge1 - edge0).abs() < f32::EPSILON {
139        return if x < edge0 { 0.0 } else { 1.0 };
140    }
141    let t = ((x - edge0) / (edge1 - edge0)).clamp(0.0, 1.0);
142    t * t * (3.0 - 2.0 * t)
143}

145#[cfg(test)]
146mod tests {
147    use super::*;
148
149    #[test]
150    fn axis_is_dormant_during_the_shrp() {
151        // At birth the axis cannot mount a response.
152        assert!(
153            hpa_competence(0.0, 0) < 0.05,
154            "newborn competence was {}",
155            hpa_competence(0.0, 0)
156        );
157    }
158
159    #[test]
160    fn axis_is_available_early_in_life() {
161        // The whole point: cortisol must exist well before the system
162        // has accumulated a large memory. Shortly after birth the axis
163        // is up.
164        assert!(
165            hpa_competence(60.0, 100) > 0.9,
166            "competence early on was {}",
167            hpa_competence(60.0, 100)
168        );
169    }
170
171    #[test]
172    fn competence_is_bounded_by_one_except_in_the_peripubertal_window() {
173        // Never unbounded, and never negative.
174        for step in 0..1000u64 {
175            let c = hpa_competence(0.0, step);
176            assert!(
177                (0.0..=MAX_COMPETENCE).contains(&c),
178                "competence {c} out of range at {step} episodes"
179            );
180        }
181    }
182
183    #[test]
184    fn adolescent_axis_is_more_responsive_than_adult() {
185        // The documented peripubertal exaggeration, sampled at the
186        // peak of the window against a clearly adult point.
187        let adolescent_episodes = (PERIPUBERTAL_CENTRE * ADULT_EPISODE_COUNT) as u64;
188        let adolescent = hpa_competence(0.0, adolescent_episodes);
189        let adult = hpa_competence(0.0, 1_000);
190        assert!(
191            adolescent > adult,
192            "adolescent {adolescent} should exceed adult {adult}"
193        );
194    }
195
196    #[test]
197    fn experience_and_elapsed_time_both_order_development() {
198        // Either clock can carry development forward, and neither can
199        // hold it back.
200        assert!(hpa_competence(0.0, 1_000) >= hpa_competence(0.0, 0));
201        assert!(hpa_competence(DEVELOPMENT_SPAN_SECS, 0) >= hpa_competence(0.0, 0));
202        // A long-lived system with no episodes still matures.
203        assert!(hpa_competence(DEVELOPMENT_SPAN_SECS * 2.0, 0) > 0.9);
204    }
205}
