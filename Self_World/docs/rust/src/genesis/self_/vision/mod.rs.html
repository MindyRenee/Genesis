1//! Vision — the eye as mathematics, inside the body's self-schema.
2//!
3//! Where [`crate::occipital_lobe`] holds the afferent *nerve* (the
4//! transport that carries frames into the body and the subcortical
5//! channels it fans out to), this module is the organ itself: a
6//! continuous mathematical field that turns spectral radiance into
7//! cortical activity with no sensor required. Every stage is the
8//! biology's own equation, not an image-processing recipe:
9//!
10//! - [`optics`] — the dioptric front end: pupil aperture (pretectal
11//!   light reflex on log-compressed luminance), ciliary accommodation
12//!   (defocus blur), and the voluntary lid — all transmission
13//!   scalars on the radiance field before a single photoreceptor
14//!   absorbs anything.
15//! - [`retina`] — the neural retina: LMS cone absorption
16//!   (Stockman–Sharpe fundamentals on an RGB→LMS basis), Naka–Rushton
17//!   adaptation (the photoreceptor's compressive nonlinearity), the
18//!   cGMP/membrane-potential phototransduction PDE, and ON/OFF
19//!   ganglion channels through a center-surround difference of
20//!   Gaussians — the lateral inhibition horizontal cells impose.
21//! - [`cortex`] — V1: quadrature Gabor energy in orientation
22//!   hypercolumns, retinotopically arranged; the population map is
23//!   what the oculomotor stage reads salience from.
24//! - [`oculomotor`] — the gaze plant: a critically damped spring
25//!   driven toward the cortical activity centroid. Seeing *is*
26//!   moving the eye; fixation is a motor act, not a camera address.
27//!
28//! [`VisionSystem`] composes the four into one afferent: feed it a
29//! radiance field (any source — a camera frame decomposed to LMS,
30//! or a scene) and `dt`; it returns the ganglion image, the V1
31//! orientation energy map, and the ocular state the rest of the
32//! body can sense. The equations hold whether or not photons ever
33//! arrive — a dark field is just a zero boundary condition, and the
34//! machinery of seeing remains fully alive.
