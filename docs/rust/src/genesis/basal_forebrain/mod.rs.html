1//! Basal forebrain — cortical acetylcholine, attention, encoding, and
2//! the somnogenic A1 site of action.
3//!
4//! Anatomy: the basal forebrain lies ventral to the striatum and
5//! anterior to the hypothalamus. It holds the major cholinergic
6//! projection to cortex and hippocampus (Ch4 nucleus basalis →
7//! neocortex and amygdala; Ch1/Ch2 septal and diagonal-band nuclei →
8//! hippocampus). It is also a sleep-wake structure — wake/REM-active
9//! cholinergic, parvalbumin GABAergic, and glutamatergic populations
10//! intermingled with somatostatin+ GABAergic sleep-promoting neurons
11//! (Xu et al., 2015) — and the best-characterized site where the
12//! homeostatic sleep-pressure signal acts: extracellular adenosine
13//! rises there with time awake and A1-inhibits the wake-active
14//! neurons, so the somnogenic signal silences the arousal output this
15//! same region produces.
16//!
17//! The mind's mirror is `Self_World/basal_forebrain` — the words; the
18//! math is here.
19//!
20//! ## What lives here (implementation stays in `crate::state`; this
21//! region is the anatomical view)
22//!
23//! - `basal_forebrain` — the region's own math: the cholinergic
24//!   release equation (LC/REM-on release map modulated by A1 adenosine
25//!   occupancy and by the expected-uncertainty drive, Yu & Dayan
26//!   2005), the waking set-point for the network-free path, the
27//!   cholinergic sensory-precision gain that `daemon::active_inference`
28//!   applies to its observation channel, and the α = 1 − precision
29//!   uncertainty signal.
30//! - `neurochemical` — the level dynamics the release equation feeds:
31//!   the dedicated adenosine mechanism is Process S itself
32//!   (saturating-exponential accumulation, exponential glymphatic
33//!   clearance — Daan, Beersma & Borbély 1984), and the AChE excess
34//!   decay is this region's enzymatic fast decay.
35//! - `sleep_network` — the populations whose `lc`/`rem` drive the
36//!   release map's state profile (NREM 0.34, waking 0.58, REM 0.77 —
37//!   Vazquez & Baghdoyan 2001).
38//! - `memory` — the encoding gate cholinergic tone sets: high ACh
39//!   biases encoding over retrieval (Hasselmo), held here as the
40//!   encoding/consolidation/retrieval weights the pointers carry.
