1//! Genesis — the core state schema and subcognitive foundation.
2//!
3//! ## Layout
4//!
5//! The organism is organized into three worlds —
6//! `Self_World/` (this crate: the physical self — brain regions,
7//! neurochemistry, the body), `Inner_World/` (the
8//! non-physical phenomena — field, waves, dreams, thoughts), and
9//! `Outer_World/` (the environment). `Self_World/src/`
10//! presents the body's anatomy the same way.
11//! The implementation lives in `daemon`, `state`, `store`,
12//! `cognition`, `field`, and `math`; the region modules
13//! (`basal_forebrain`, `basal_ganglia`, `brainstem`, `cerebellum`,
14//! `frontal_lobe`, `hypothalamus`, `insula`, `limbic_system`,
15//! `memory`, `neurochemical`, `occipital_lobe`, `parietal_lobe`,
16//! `self_`, `sleep`, `temporal_lobe`, `thalamus`, `tools`,
17//! `infrastructure`, `worlds`) re-export those modules under the
18//! structure they implement, so the crate reads the same way the
19//! mind does: click `src/` and the regions are what you see.
20
