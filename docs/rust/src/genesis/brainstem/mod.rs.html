1//! Brainstem — the autonomic core of the body.
2//!
3//! Anatomy:
4//! arousal and visceral reflexes, interacting with hypothalamic homeostasis.
5//! It also houses the monoaminergic nuclei that broadcast
6//! neuromodulators upward (locus coeruleus → norepinephrine, raphe →
7//! serotonin, VTA/SN → dopamine), and the reticular activating
8//! system that sets arousal zones. In Genesis the daemon's own clock
9//! and body-maintenance loop is this region: it ticks whether or not
10//! the cognitive mind is alive.
11//!
12//! ## What lives here (implementation stays in `crate::daemon` /
13//! `crate::state`; this region is the anatomical view)
14//!
15//! - `tick` — the pacemaker. Sole authority on time and physics:
16//!   integrates neurochemistry, circadian phase, and sleep staging at
17//!   `TICK_INTERVAL_MS`, lease-driven by the mind while it lives.
18//! - `interoception` — visceral sensing of the machine body: CPU,
19//!   thermal, battery, disk. The body's read on its own condition.
20//! - `cpufreq` — autonomic frequency/thermal policy (the brainstem's
21//!   homeostatic reflexes, applied to silicon).
22//! - `active_inference` — the body's anticipatory regulation:
23//!   free energy, precision, surprise, allostatic load — published as
24//!   `inference` signals the mind observes rather than drives.
25//! - `zones` — arousal/task zone state (the reticular activating
26//!   system's projection target).
27//! - `core_state`, `header`, `sanitize`, `legacy_v3` — the somatic
28//!   ground truth: the fixed-layout body state every region shares,
29//!   its integrity checks, and the frozen prior schema.
30
