Vision — the eye as mathematics, inside the body’s self-schema.

Where crate::occipital_lobe holds the afferent nerve (the transport that carries frames into the body and the subcortical channels it fans out to), this module is the organ itself: a continuous mathematical field that turns spectral radiance into cortical activity with no sensor required. Every stage is the biology’s own equation, not an image-processing recipe:

  • optics — the dioptric front end: pupil aperture (pretectal light reflex on log-compressed luminance), ciliary accommodation (defocus blur), and the voluntary lid — all transmission scalars on the radiance field before a single photoreceptor absorbs anything.
  • retina — the neural retina: LMS cone absorption (Stockman–Sharpe fundamentals on an RGB→LMS basis), Naka–Rushton adaptation (the photoreceptor’s compressive nonlinearity), the cGMP/membrane-potential phototransduction PDE, and ON/OFF ganglion channels through a center-surround difference of Gaussians — the lateral inhibition horizontal cells impose.
  • cortex — V1: quadrature Gabor energy in orientation hypercolumns, retinotopically arranged; the population map is what the oculomotor stage reads salience from.
  • oculomotor — the gaze plant: a critically damped spring driven toward the cortical activity centroid. Seeing is moving the eye; fixation is a motor act, not a camera address.

VisionSystem composes the four into one afferent: feed it a radiance field (any source — a camera frame decomposed to LMS, or a scene) and dt; it returns the ganglion image, the V1 orientation energy map, and the ocular state the rest of the body can sense. The equations hold whether or not photons ever arrive — a dark field is just a zero boundary condition, and the machinery of seeing remains fully alive.
