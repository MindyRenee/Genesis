Thalamus — the relay and integration layer.

Anatomy: the thalamus is the brain’s relay station — every sensory channel except olfaction passes through thalamic nuclei on the way to cortex, and thalamocortical loops broadcast and bind activity across regions. In Genesis the body↔mind boundary crosses here: the Unix-socket IPC protocol is the fiber tract, the module manifest is the relay’s routing table, and the field runtime is the integrative dynamics the broadcast rides on.

§What lives here

  • ipc — the wire protocol between daemon and cognitive mind (PROTOCOL_VERSION, opcodes, request/reply framing). The geniculate bodies of the architecture: the mind’s only line to the body runs through it.
  • manifest — the module registry: which subsystems are alive, their status, CPU share. The relay knows its connections.
  • engine, runtime — the field architecture: every subsystem a layer of the system’s latent field, gauge transport between them. The integrative substrate the tick loop drives.
