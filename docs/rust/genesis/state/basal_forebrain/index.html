Basal forebrain — the cholinergic output equation and its modulators.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ ANATOMY ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The basal forebrain lies ventral to the striatum and anterior to the hypothalamus. It holds the major cholinergic projection to the cortex and hippocampus: Ch1/Ch2 (medial septum, vertical diagonal band) supply the hippocampal complex, Ch4 (nucleus basalis of Meynert) supplies neocortex and amygdala. It is also a sleep-wake structure: three intermingled populations — wake/REM-active cholinergic, parvalbumin GABAergic, and glutamatergic neurons organized in a Glu→Ch→PV excitatory hierarchy — are all inhibited by somatostatin+ GABAergic sleep-promoting neurons (Xu et al., Nat. Neurosci. 2015), and it is the best-characterized site where the homeostatic sleep signal acts: extracellular adenosine rises there with time awake (Porkka-Heiskanen et al., Science 1997) and A1-receptor activation inhibits the region’s wake-active neurons (Strecker et al., J. Neurosci. 2000; Basheer et al., 2004). The sleep signal literally silences the arousal output this same region produces.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ THE OUTPUT EQUATION ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The region’s release level is a function of the sleep-wake network’s populations plus two modulators:

target = ACH_BASE + ACH_FROM_LC · lc · (1 − A1) · D(α) + ACH_FROM_REM · rem

  • The linear map of lc (wake) and rem (REM-on) reproduces the microdialysis steady states — NREM 0.34, waking 0.58, REM 0.77 (Vazquez & Baghdoyan 2001) — see state::neurochemical for the calibrated coefficients.
  • A1 is adenosine-A1-receptor occupancy on the wake-active cholinergic population: the somnogenic signal attenuates only the LC-driven component, so the calibrated sleep profiles (which have no LC drive) are preserved exactly while waking cortical ACh sags as pressure builds.
  • D(α) is the expected-uncertainty drive (Yu & Dayan, Neuron 2005): acetylcholine reports expected uncertainty — the brain’s estimate of how unreliable its own predictions are — so the wake component is scaled up when confidence is low and down when it is high.

The efferent edge is also here: sensory_precision_gain is the cholinergic gain on bottom-up prediction-error precision — ACh optimizes the expected precision of sensory channels by gain on supragranular pyramidal cells (Feldman & Friston, Front. Hum. Neurosci. 2010; Moran et al., J. Neurosci. 2013), implemented in daemon::active_inference as a divisor on observation variance.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════ WHAT THIS MODULE IS NOT ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Process S itself (adenosine accumulation and glymphatic clearance), the AChE excess decay, the coupling matrix rows, and the network release of the other chemicals live in state::neurochemical; the populations that drive lc/rem live in state::sleep_network. This module owns only the basal forebrain’s own math — what reads those signals and what they do to its output.

Constants§

A1_EC50
Effective adenosine level for half-maximal A1 occupancy.
A1_MAX_INHIBITION
Maximum fraction of the LC-driven release component that A1 occupancy can suppress. Capped at one half: A1 activation inhibits firing of the wake-active cholinergic neurons but does not silence the population outright (Strecker et al., 2000).
A1_OCCUPANCY_AT_REST 🔒
A1 occupancy at the resting level — the zero point of a1_suppression. Const-evaluable from the Hill form.
ACH_BASE
Basal (state-independent) term of the release map.
ACH_FROM_LC
Weight of the LC (wake) population’s drive on cholinergic release.
ACH_FROM_REM
Weight of the REM-on population’s drive on cholinergic release.
ACH_PRECISION_GAIN
Gain of ACh deviation-from-baseline on sensory precision.
ADENOSINE_REST 🔒
The resting extracellular adenosine level — the chemical’s baseline and Process S’s lower asymptote. Suppression is measured relative to occupancy at this level so rest is exactly neutral.
SENSORY_PRECISION_GAIN_MAX
SENSORY_PRECISION_GAIN_MIN
Bounds on the precision multiplier. Attention shifts the weight of evidence; it does not open or close the channel.
UNCERTAINTY_GAIN
Gain of the expected-uncertainty drive on the wake component. 0.15 moves the LC-driven term by at most ~±13% across the whole α range — attention modulates the state profile, it does not set it.
UNCERTAINTY_REFERENCE
Expected uncertainty at the well-predicted operating point — the reference where the uncertainty drive is exactly neutral. Resting precision sits at ~0.9 (the precision tracker’s resting target), so resting α ≈ 0.1.
