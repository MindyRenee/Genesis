Sleep-wake regulatory network: wake, NREM, and REM populations with a homeostatic sleep drive and a circadian oscillator.

Transcribed from the MATLAB source published with ModelDB entry 244485 (DinizBehnBooth_SWnetworkModel.m, run_DinizBehnBooth_SWnetworkModel.m). That implements Gleit, Diniz Behn & Booth, J. Biol. Rhythms 28:339–355 (2013), as revised in Booth, Xique & Diniz Behn, SIAM J. Appl. Dyn. Sys. 16(2):1089–1112 (2017).

Populations: f_lc (wake, locus coeruleus), f_vlpo (NREM / sleep, ventrolateral preoptic area), f_r (REM-on, sublaterodorsal/precoeruleus), f_scn (circadian pacemaker).

Sleep onset is f_lc falling below SLEEP_THRESHOLD; REM onset is f_r rising above REM_THRESHOLD. Both are the source’s own event definitions. Time is in minutes.

scheduled_light 🔒
The built-in photoperiod — the world’s fallback light signal when no retinal measurement exists. 500 lux during the 14 h light period, else 0. The source runs light from minute 600 of each simulated day (10:00–24:00); here t is anchored to the local wall clock, so the window is phase-shifted to 07:00–21:00 — a 14 h photoperiod, preserved — so that the entrained sleep episode (~22:11–05:50) falls inside the neurochemical circadian’s biological night rather than ending ~2 h after melatonin offset as it did with the source’s alignment.
sigmoid 🔒
stage
The sleep stage the network implies, by the source’s own definitions: wake while LC fires at or above SLEEP_THRESHOLD; otherwise REM when REM-on fires above REM_THRESHOLD, and NREM when it does not.
switch_fixed_points 🔒
Every fixed point of the reduced switch at homeostat h, in ascending order — the roots of switch_map, located by sign change on a grid and refined by bisection. One low root is the sleep branch, one high root is the wake branch, three roots are both branches plus the saddle between them.
switch_map 🔒
The scalar fixed-point map of the reduced (LC, VLPO) subsystem: g(f) = LC∞(I_LC(f_vlpo*(f))) − f with the homeostat substituted for the state’s own x[4] and the REM/circadian drives frozen at the state’s current values. A root of g is a fixed point of the switch; where g’s slope at the root is negative the fixed point is a stable branch.
switch_root_count 🔒
The number of fixed points of the reduced switch at homeostat h.
