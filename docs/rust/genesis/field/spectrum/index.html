Spectral observables of the field’s own dynamics.

The field substrate is her computational medium: three fibers — body, prediction, agency — drift, couple, and fluctuate on the latent manifold under gauge transport and a Langevin thermostat. This module is the band decomposition of that trajectory: the same center frequencies the cortical wave model reports, measured off the field’s own motion rather than assumed.

Two channels mirror the substrate’s two honest observables:

  • flux — the speed of the fibers’ centroid through latent space (|Δmean|/h per integration substep). The field’s motion energy per band: how much of its wandering lives at each rhythm.
  • tension — the fibers’ mean deviation from their own centroid (latent-coordinate spread). The disagreement’s spectral shadow: where the field’s internal argument oscillates.

The carrier block tracks the consensus vector’s own kinematics — displacement speed, winding rate, accumulated winding phase — the same measured quantities daemon::maxwell evaluates, kept here so the spectrum and the signature read one trajectory.

Sampling is the field’s own integration rate: observe substeps the coupling at SPECTRUM_STEP_HZ, so the rings resolve the modeled continuous dynamics — the same ODEs integrated finer, which is more accurate, not a second instrument. Bands above the achieved rate’s Nyquist bound are zeroed: an unresolvable band is an absent channel, never an aliased reading.

Constants§

BASELINE_ALPHA 🔒
Baseline EMA rate per signature read — band energies are reported relative to the channel’s own recent history (≈0.125 typical on a quiet field), so a sustained elevation reads as elevation rather than as an absolute that means nothing across hosts.
CARRIER_ALPHA 🔒
Consensus-kinematics EMA rate per observe — the carrier readings smooth over ~7 integrations (~1.4 s at the 5 Hz tick).
FIELD_BANDS
Band center frequencies (Hz) — the cortical wave model’s own bands, in wire order: infraslow, delta, theta, alpha, sigma, beta1, beta2, beta3, gamma, epsilon.
RATE_ALPHA 🔒
Achieved-rate EMA rate per observe.
RING_LEN 🔒
Ring depth — 4096 samples ≈ 16.4 s at the nominal rate: ~4 cycles of infraslow, hundreds of the fast bands.
SPECTRUM_STEP_HZ
Internal integration/sampling rate (Hz). The field’s coupling substeps at this cadence inside each observe; 250 Hz resolves every band through gamma and leaves epsilon (150 Hz) honestly above Nyquist — the same coverage boundary the substrate’s own dynamics can support.
WARM_SAMPLES 🔒
Samples before the spectrum reports warm — ~4 s: enough for the fast bands to resolve; the slow bands keep accumulating baseline afterward. Below this the signature’s presence flag stays clear.

band_phase 🔒
Quadrature phase of samples at freq — the DFT bin’s angle, atan2(−Σx·sin, Σx·cos) at the exact frequency (not the Goertzel bin), the phase a band-limited rhythm actually carries.
band_power 🔒
Goertzel power of samples at freq sampled at fs — the same second-order recurrence the wave model’s own band meter runs: s₀ = x + coeff·s₁ − s₂, coeff = 2·cos(2πk/n) at the bin nearest freq; power = (s₁² + s₂² − coeff·s₁·s₂)/n².
