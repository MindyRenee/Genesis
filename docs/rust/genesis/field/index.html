Expand description

Machine-native field architecture for Genesis.

A major system is a SystemFiber; its subsystems are the layers of that system’s latent field. Geometry is separated from semantics: a manifold is a container and metric, while layers own represented state and dynamics.

The latent manifold is the κ-stereographic sphere — the constant positive curvature counterpart of the Poincaré ball (Bachmann et al. 2020): the sphere of curvature κ projected stereographically onto the chart, with the north pole at the origin. The field operates inside the hemisphere cap ‖x‖ < 1/√κ, a geodesically convex region whose center is the pole — the organism’s nominal home.

The implementation is dependency-free and deterministic. It does not assume that a fixed number of fibers is correct.

KICK_LATENT_SCALE 🔒
The latent working box’s half-width — the thermal kick samples temperature as a fraction of the field’s operating extent. Unscaled, σ ≈ √(2·gain·T·dt) reaches ~0.45 tangent units on a hot field while the whole box spans ±0.5 — a kick that size scatters the fibers across the manifold instead of perturbing them, and the scattered fibers re-create the disagreement that keeps the temperature pinned: a thermostat stuck on. Scaling σ by the box half-width makes the kick a genuine fluctuation and lets a cooling field actually settle.
