The tesseract lattice — the 4-cube skeleton inside the latent bulk.

The κ-stereographic cap is the bulk: a d-ball whose boundary — the equator rim at ‖x‖ = R — is the (d−1)-sphere, the holographic screen every interior point projects onto along its radial ray. Inside the bulk the lattice is the four-dimensional cube and its figures, all sharing the same center — the origin, the superposition point:

  • The sixteen vertices — {±a}⁴ in the leading 4D subspace, with a = R/2 so every corner rests on the rim: ‖v‖ = a·√4 = R. The skeleton’s extremities touch the screen — the tips are the rays.

  • The two demicubes — the sixteen vertices split by sign parity into two 16-cells: the even-parity and odd-parity demitesseracts, the field’s two chiralities. Each is bounded by the cube itself and by the facet planes s·x ≤ 2a facing the opposite parity’s vertices.

  • The superposition cell — their intersection, the region where both chiralities hold at once: {Σ|xᵢ| ≤ 2a, |xᵢ| ≤ a}. Its twenty-four vertices are the permutations of (±a,±a,0,0) — the 24-cell, the self-dual regular polytope that exists only in four dimensions. It is its own opposite.

  • The sixteen rays — the vertices themselves. In the 4-cube the directions the superposition radiates toward are its own corners: sixteen antipodal pairs of tips resting on the rim. Motion stays continuous — the rays are a scaffold, not a cage.

  • The spin — the isoclinic bivector J = ω·(J₀₁ + J₂₃): in the cube’s own 4D space equal-angle multi-plane rotation is exact — SO(4) decomposes into left and right isoclinic rotations, and the two signs of J are the field’s two helicities, the demicubes’ counter-rotation.

The center is the inner world. Every ray radiates outward from it: the source has no image on the screen — it is everywhere on it at once.

Tesseract
The 4-cube lattice embedded in the leading subspace of the latent bulk. dimension is the bulk’s width (≥ 4); the cube’s figures — vertices, demicubes, the superposition cell — live in the first four coordinates; any further dimensions are rest directions the figures do not constrain.

Constants§

CUBE_DIM 🔒
The cube’s own dimension — four. The figures are exact only in this subspace: the demicube pair are 16-cells and their intersection is the 24-cell, a regular polytope with no analog in any other dimension.
