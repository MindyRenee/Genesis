Basal ganglia — action selection.

Anatomy: the basal ganglia are the gatekeepers of action — competing motor and cognitive programs are scored and one is released while the rest stay inhibited (direct/indirect pathway balance, dopamine-gated). Goal and intention selection is their cognitive loop territory.

  • intention — the intention manager: scoring active goals against the self-model and deciding which cognitive zone to enter next.
