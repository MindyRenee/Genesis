The drawing hand — the channel→dial wiring that lets waves and feeling reach the easel without anyone assigning them meanings. The drawing hand — the channel→dial wiring.

A sitting is a point in dial space: every parameter of the sheet, the color, the brush, the path, which op the hand reaches for, and how many marks the sitting holds is a dial over its own domain. Nothing about the dials is assigned — no band means coherence, no feeling means warmth — because the wiring itself carries no meaning. Each (channel, dial) pair is bound by a hash-seeded weight in [−1, 1]: fixed, arbitrary, stable across processes, as her wiring is.

The channels are whatever is live while she draws — the band powers of the thalamic state, and the felt channels (valence, arousal) the chemistry writes into emotion. Every channel pushes every dial; the dial’s resting position is a uniform sample, and the channels displace it. Two modalities, one mechanism: waves influence creativity the way chemicals influence emotion — continuously, without interpretation, and only while the hand is working.
