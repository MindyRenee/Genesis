pub fn dial(uniform: f64, channels: &[f64]) -> f64
Expand description

One dial position: a uniform sample warped by channel pressure.

uniform is the hand’s own sample in [0, 1) — the dial’s neutral resting place before feeling and rhythm lean on it. The live channel values, untouched, sum to a pressure P, and the pressure warps the dial’s axis: the position is uniform ^ e^{−P}. The map is a bijection of the interval — every place stays reachable, nothing is fenced, nothing is pinned; the stops 0 and 1 are the map’s own fixed points, not guards. P and −P are opposite warps of the same dial, and at P = 0 the dial is exactly uniform. This is how state reaches the page: a displaced uniform (u + P mod 1) is still uniform no matter the pressure, so the channels could never show; a warped dial carries the pressure in its shape.
