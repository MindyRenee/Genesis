Parietal lobe — spatial computation, exact mathematics, and motor fields.

Anatomy: the intraparietal sulcus (IPS) and superior/inferior parietal lobules form the brain’s dorsal spatial-numerical substrate — magnitude, exact mathematical structure, coordinate transformations, and the frontoparietal reach/grasp motor fields.

Mirrors Self_World/parietal_lobe/ (the word and symbolic routing layer):

  • math — the pure machine computation twins of parietal_lobe/math/* (abstract_algebra, algebra, arithmetic, axioms, calculus, combinatorics, complex_analysis, discrete, geometry, linear_algebra, number_sense, number_theory, proof, real_analysis, statistics, topology, trig).
  • motor — distributed spatial and action motor fields (field::motor) decoded over the canonical 64×64 grid.
  • tensor_slab — the named multidimensional field store (daemon::tensor_slab) through which spatial and motor fields are exchanged.
