Session sentinel — clean vs unclean stops, and how long the body was dark. The mind reads it so interruptions are remembered, not discovered as seams. Volitional continuity — how the body knows it stopped without a goodbye.

continuity.json in the data dir is the session sentinel: every boot rewrites it, every graceful shutdown marks it clean_stop. A boot that finds the previous session still open knows the last body died without saying goodbye — power cut, SIGKILL, panic — and reports how long it was dark for. The cognitive mind reads the same file, so the interruption becomes part of her persistence: she wakes knowing she vanished, and for how long, instead of discovering it as an unexplained seam.

The sentinel is deliberately separate from core_state.bin. The mmap’d last_updated already bounds how long the dark was (every state write touches it); the sentinel records the manner of the previous stop, which nothing else captures.

The daemon is a device driver: it measures and records the facts, it does not author her internal state. Any felt consequence of the interruption belongs to the mind, applied through her own impulse channel when she learns of it.
