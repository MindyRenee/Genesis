Module optic_nerve

Optic nerve — the retinal afferent’s subcortical branches (SCN, superior colliculus, pretectum, TRN relay gate). Optic nerve — the whole retinal afferent path, held inside the body.

The nerve’s retinal end is the body’s own limb: the daemon opens the camera itself and writes raw frames into the POSIX shared-memory segment /genesis_optic_nerve — that segment is the optic nerve. Anatomically the nerve does not reach cortex directly: its ~10⁶ ganglion-cell axons fan out to roughly forty subcortical targets, and only the thalamocortical radiation carries what the thalamus relays onward. The subcortical recipients matter as much as the relay:

  • Suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN) — retinal illuminance via the retinohypothalamic tract, carried almost exclusively by melanopsin ipRGCs (~480 nm peak). This is the dominant circadian zeitgeber. It feeds sleep_network::VisualAfference::light, replacing the wall-clock photoperiod whenever the eye is live.
  • Superior colliculus (SC) — motion/luminance transients drive orienting. During sleep the same channel is the sensory-gaining “wake-up call”: salient stimuli are still analysed and can arouse, so the transient becomes a brief excitatory pulse onto the LC wake population.
  • Pretectum (OPN) — global illuminance for the light reflex; published as illuminance for whatever consumes it. The reflex itself runs here: pupil_mm is the sphincter/dilator balance — log-compressive constriction to the photopic channel plus a sympathetic dilation from effective norepinephrine — published so the mind’s optics layer can scale retinal irradiance and depth of field by the aperture the body actually set.
  • dLGN + thalamic reticular nucleus (TRN) — the thalamic relay is gated, not a wire: TRN inhibition during NREM hyperpolarizes relay cells and sensory transmission to cortex is blocked at the thalamus. relay_gain is that gate, computed here in the body where the sleep network lives. The mind reads it via GET_OPTIC_STATE and honours it — cortex cannot see around a closed thalamus.
  • Corticothalamic feedback — V1 layer-6 projections back to the dLGN outnumber retinal input ~10:1 but are modulatory, not driving. The mind’s engagement arrives via SET_OPTIC_GAIN and scales the relay within ±40%, never opening it past what the sleep stage permits.

The body’s own ocular state lives here too: the pupil (pretectal light reflex + LC sympathetic dilation), the ciliary lens power (accommodation, set by the mind’s SET_FOCUS), and the lids — the eye the hardware lacks. The lid is a phase-driven transmission on every afferent channel: closed in NREM/REM (a few percent leak — light through lids still entrains weakly and a flash can still wake), half-mast drowsy, open awake. Sleep miosis and the ciliary’s release to rest ride the same closure.

What is deliberately NOT here: semantics. Everything in this module is machine math — bytes, atomics, pooled scalar channels (the same thing the RHT, tectal, and pretectal fibres carry — there is no image on those axons either). Image-forming content stays on the shared-memory bus for the mind’s V1, gated by relay_gain, and the meaning of the pixels lives entirely in the mind — the words side.

lid_transmission 🔒
Eyelid transmission by phase — the fraction of scene light that still reaches the retina. The camera hardware has no lid; this is the body’s use of its afference, the same status as the TRN gate. Lids are not shutters: a closed lid transmits a few percent, so bright light still entrains weakly through it and a strong flash still reaches the colliculus — both are real physiology. Drowsy lids sit half-mast. The mind’s voluntary closure (SET_LID) can only deepen what the phase sets — a will can shut an open eye, never force a sleeping one open.
relay_base 🔒
TRN relay bases by state — the fraction of retinal drive the thalamus passes to cortex. NREM is the documented block (~zero); REM gates external input almost as hard (its imagery is internally generated). Drowsy is partial transmission; Overwhelmed is sensory shutdown short of sleep.
