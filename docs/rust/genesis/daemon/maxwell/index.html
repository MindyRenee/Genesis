Maxwell’s equations applied to the measured carrier current. Maxwell’s equations applied to the measured carrier current.

The field probe writes a rhythm into real work; that work is real current — charge moving through the die’s power network. Maxwell’s equations say what field that current makes. This module evaluates them for our regime rather than leaving “electromagnetic” as a figure of speech.

§The equations and the regime

∇·E = ρ/ε₀        ∇·B = 0
∇×E = −∂B/∂t      ∇×B = μ₀J + μ₀ε₀∂E/∂t

A current loop carrying I(t) has magnetic moment m(t) = I(t)·A. The exact retarded solution for an oscillating magnetic dipole expands in powers of 1/r (Jackson, Classical Electrodynamics; Griffiths, Introduction to Electrodynamics):

B(r,t) = μ₀/4π · [ 3n̂(m·n̂) − m           (quasi-static, ∝1/r³)
                 + 3n̂(ṁ·n̂) − ṁ / (cr²)    (induction,    ∝ω/cr²)
                 + (m̈×n̂)×n̂ / (c²r) ]     (radiation,   ∝ω²/c²r)
E(r,t) = −μ₀/4π · [ ṁ/r² + m̈/(cr) ] × n̂

evaluated at retarded time t₀ = t − r/c.

At the carrier’s frequencies (ω ≲ 10³ rad/s) and any terrestrial distance, ωr/c ≲ 10⁻⁵ — the induction and radiation terms are ~10⁻¹⁰ and ~10⁻²⁰ of the quasi-static term. Maxwell’s equations themselves say so: a slowly varying current makes a magnetostatic field that sloshes in place (energy leaves and returns each cycle), not a wave. The displacement-current term ε₀∂E/∂t — Maxwell’s own addition to Ampère’s law — is what would carry energy away, and at these frequencies it contributes nothing measurable. The equations do not flatter the carrier; they bound it honestly.

What survives is Ampère’s law: the carrier’s current swing makes a real, oscillating magnetic field at nano-tesla scale a foot from the machine. Real — and, as the equations also show, unmeasurably faint without a magnetometer.

§Model status

Inputs are measured (carrier current amplitude from real power swing over a real rail, carrier frequency from the generator). The loop area is a documented order-of-magnitude assumption (die/package current loop ~1 cm²). Outputs are therefore estimates of field magnitude, labeled as such on the wire — the field Maxwell’s equations predict from what we measure, not a field we sense. EM emanation from running silicon is real and measured in the literature (Yilmaz, Prvulović & Zajić, IEEE TIFS 2019; power-delivery dI/dt noise via near-field probes, IEEE MICRO 2018) — at MHz–GHz. At 10 Hz the same physics applies with the honest ω⁴-suppressed radiation the equations return.

quasi_static_ok
Whether the quasi-static truncation is honest at (ω, r): ωr/c ≪ 1, so the dropped induction/radiation terms are <1% of the terms we keep.

Time-averaged radiated power of an oscillating magnetic dipole, ⟨P⟩ = μ₀m₀²ω⁴/(12πc³) — the radiation-zone integral of the Poynting vector (m₀ = peak moment). Watts.
