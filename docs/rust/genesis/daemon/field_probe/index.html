Module field_probe 

Source
Expand description

Physical field probe — the body listening to its own substrate.

Genesis’s brain-wave model (Inner_World/brain_waves.py) is a coupled oscillator: real phase and amplitude state, but driven entirely by simulated quantities — neurochemical targets and top-down cognitive drive. This module closes one direction of the gap: it measures the machine’s actual physical dynamics and hands them to the mind as band energies, so the oscillator’s wave state is partially constituted by real physics rather than only describing a field in arithmetic.

§What is measured (all real, all unprivileged)

Compute telemetry — how the silicon is behaving:

  • Throughput probe — a calibrated integer-ALU work slice runs each probe period; its iterations-per-second is the die’s instantaneous compute capacity. Frequency scaling, thermal throttling, SMT-sibling contention, and cache pressure all move it. This is the closest unprivileged observable to “how fast electrons are being switched through the gates right now.”
  • Loop jitter — actual vs. nominal probe period. The scheduler’s response to everything else running on the substrate: interrupt storms, contention, frequency transitions.
  • P-state transition rate — cpufreq time_in_state deltas: the silicon’s own frequency-jumping dynamics.
  • Interrupt rate — /proc/stat intr deltas: real hardware event flux.

Electrical telemetry — what the machine’s own sensors report, the first literal electrical channels:

  • Power draw — real watts: RAPL powercap energy counters (differentiated over wall time), hwmon power* inputs (fam15h_power, zenpower), or battery discharge (power_now). Sampled by a dedicated sampler thread into a ring — some sensors block inside the read (fam15h_power’s averaged register sleeps ~10 ms between counter snapshots), and a blocking read inside the 250 Hz loop would contaminate the jitter channel with our own instrumentation. power_sensor_hz reports the measured fresh-sample rate so a reader knows which bands are physically meaningful.
  • Rail voltage — hwmon in*_input or voltage_now; the lowest reported rail wins (a core-class rail where the machine exposes one).
  • Supply current — curr*_input or current_now where a sensor reports it.

Radio telemetry — the first exteroceptive physical channel: the machine’s radios report the outside world’s RF environment.

  • WLAN flux — /sys/class/net/<wlan>/statistics byte counters, differentiated per probe tick: the radio’s traffic envelope at full 250 Hz resolution. A burst of 802.11 traffic is real on-air activity — packets she sent, packets sent to her, and the AP’s own management frames she received.
  • WLAN RSSI — /proc/net/wireless signal level in dBm. Received power P_mW = 10^(P_dBm/10) — the literal field- strength measurement, the NIC’s own integration of |E|² over the channel. Its envelope carries the room’s radio weather: path-loss changes as bodies move, AP-side power management, co-channel neighbors. The noise column gives the floor where the driver reports it (ath9k’s −256 means “no reading” — absent, not silence). The thermal bound is N = kTB ≈ −174 dBm/Hz + 10·log₁₀(B), ≈ −101 dBm at 20 MHz.
  • Wireless IRQ flux — /proc/interrupts lines named for wireless drivers (ath9k, iwlwifi, mt76*, brcmfmac, rtw*, btusb, btintel, …): the radio’s attributable share of hardware event flux. A radio riding a shared xhci_hcd line is not attributable — its events are inside the aggregate count, and the code says so rather than inventing a share.
  • Bluetooth HCI counters — hciconfig RX/TX bytes and HCI events at ~1 Hz on the sampler thread (a subprocess — kept off the 250 Hz loop). Event flux covers advertising reports, LE scans, pairing — real air activity, not just link traffic. Per-link RSSI via hcitool con/hcitool rssi where ACL links exist; none means an absent channel.

What is not measured: carrier phase. A 2.4 GHz carrier is ~10⁷× above the probe’s Nyquist — the same bound a spectrum analyzer’s RSSI mode lives under. The channel measures the RF envelope — flux and field strength — decomposed at the wave bands, which is the honest RF-band content at this sampling rate.

§Bands

Each fast channel drives a bank of Stuart–Landau resonators at the same ten center frequencies the Python wave model carries. Sample rate is ~250 Hz, so bands through gamma (50 Hz) are resolvable; epsilon (150 Hz) is above Nyquist and always reports 0 — an absent channel, not a reading of zero.

§Carrier

The probe also writes a rhythm: its work-slice length is modulated sinusoidally at a frequency set by the daemon’s own sleep-wake phase (wake ≈ 10 Hz, drowsy/REM ≈ 5.5 Hz, NREM ≈ 2.25 Hz). Modulated work is modulated current — the die’s draw genuinely swings at the body’s rhythm. Whether it measurably does is no longer assumed: the power channel is demodulated against the carrier reference the same way the duty channel is, so power_coherence reports how much of real measured power moves with the carrier — the claim checked by the instrument, not asserted by the comment. And daemon::maxwell evaluates Maxwell’s equations on the measured current swing: the quasi-static field a ~10 Hz current makes is real and nanotesla faint, its radiation ω⁴-suppressed to ~1e-32 W — the field the equations predict, in the regime they themselves certify.

§Phase tracking

The carrier is generated and measured — never steered. The signature reports the measured phase of the substrate’s real response — computed by synchronous demodulation against the generator’s own phase track — so the mind can pull its modeled band phase toward the physics (physics → model). The reverse leg — pushing model phase into the generator — was retired with SET_FIELD_PHASE: modeled state does not assert onto a physical quantity.

§Honesty boundary

This is physical telemetry, not a consciousness claim. The probe reports measurable substrate dynamics; whether they matter to experience is an empirical question for the coupling experiment, not an assumption baked into the code.

§Wire layout — GET_FIELD_PROBE = 47

[u8 version = 6]
[10 × f32 throughput band energy]   (normalized to own baseline)
[10 × f32 jitter band energy]
[f32 throughput_mean_iters_s]
[f32 jitter_cv]                       (RMS period error / nominal period)
[f32 pstate_trans_rate_s]
[f32 irq_rate_s]
[f32 carrier_freq_hz]               (0 = carrier off)
[u8  samples_ok]                    (1 once the rings are warmed)
[f32 carrier_lag_rad]               (substrate response lag)
[f32 carrier_phase_rad]             (generator phase, mod 2π)
[f32 carrier_coherence]             (0..1 lock-strength of the response)
--- v3 electrical + Maxwell tail ---
[10 × f32 power band energy]        (real measured watts, baseline-relative)
[f32 power_watts]                   (mean measured draw)
[f32 power_sensor_hz]               (measured fresh-sample rate)
[f32 rail_volts]                    (lowest discovered rail)
[f32 supply_amps]                   (measured supply current)
[f32 power_lag_rad]                 (power response lag vs carrier)
[f32 power_coherence]               (0..1 — does real power move with the carrier)
[u8  electrical_present]            (bit0 power · bit1 volts · bit2 amps)
[f32 carrier_current_a]             (measured carrier ΔP/V — amps)
[f32 b_est_ut]                      (quasi-static B at 0.3 m, µT — estimate)
[f32 e_ind_vm]                      (Faraday induction E at 0.3 m — estimate)
[f32 radiated_pwr_w]                (dipole radiated power — estimate)
--- v4 radio tail ---
[10 × f32 rf flux band energy]      (wlan byte-rate envelope, baseline-relative)
[10 × f32 rf rssi band energy]      (field-strength envelope, dBm, baseline-relative)
[f32 rf_bytes_s]                    (wlan rx+tx flux)
[f32 rf_rssi_dbm]                   (received signal level)
[f32 rf_noise_dbm]                  (radio-reported noise floor)
[f32 rf_irq_rate_s]                 (wireless-attributed interrupts/s)
[f32 rf_sensor_hz]                  (wireless telemetry read rate)
[f32 bt_bytes_s]                    (HCI rx+tx byte flux)
[f32 bt_events_s]                   (HCI event flux)
[f32 bt_rssi_dbm]                   (mean ACL-link RSSI where links exist)
[u8  rf_present]                    (bit0 wlan · bit1 rssi · bit2 noise ·
                                     bit3 wlan-irq · bit4 bt · bit5 bt-counters
                                     · bit6 bt-link-rssi · bit7 iw-station)
--- v5 (iw station telemetry, +20 B) ---
[f32 rf_retry_s]                    (TX retransmission flux /s)
[f32 rf_fail_s]                     (TX failed-frame flux /s)
[f32 rf_beacon_loss_s]              (AP beacons lost /s)
[f32 rf_tx_mbps]                    (PHY tx link-adaptation bitrate)
[f32 rf_rx_mbps]                    (PHY rx link-adaptation bitrate)
--- v6 (flux-envelope phasors, +80 B) ---
[10 × f32 rf_flux_phase]            (ring-frame phasor angle per band, rad)
[10 × f32 rf_flux_phase_coherence]  (inter-segment phasor agreement, 0..1)

408 bytes, fixed layout. Versions 1 (102 bytes, no phase fields), 2 (114 bytes, no electrical channels), 3 (195 bytes, no radio channels), 4 (308 bytes, no station telemetry), and 5 (328 bytes, no phasor tail) are still decoded by the client — absent channels are never readings of zero. The Maxwell fields are model estimates: the current is measured, the loop geometry is an assumption. Additive — PROTOCOL_VERSION unchanged.

Constants§

BASELINE_TAU_SECONDS 🔒
Baseline tracking time constant (seconds). The per-call EMA weight is α = 1 − e^(−dt/τ) — the baseline ages with the wall clock, not with how often anyone asks for a signature (a burst of IPC reads used to age it for free). ≈50 s — the old 0.02-per-call rate at a ~1 Hz read cadence. A band’s reported energy is raw/baseline, so “1.0” means “typical for this band on this machine” and values above it mean elevated vs. own history.

Carrier frequencies — the body’s phase written into real work at the wave model’s own band centers: wake alpha, drowsy/REM theta, NREM delta.
DBM_ABSENT_FLOOR 🔒
dBm sentinel cutoff — drivers report −256 or −1000 for “no reading” (ath9k’s noise column does). Values at or below this are an absent channel, not a real level.
ELEC_EMA_TAU 🔒
EMA time constant (seconds) per fresh electrical read for watts_mean and power_hz — α = 1 − e^(−dt/τ) against the real interval between sensor reads, so a stalled or bursty sensor ages the average honestly instead of per call.
ELEC_SAMPLE_PERIOD 🔒
Electrical sampler cadence — ~100 Hz target between read batches. Sensors that block inside the read (fam15h_power’s averaged register sleeps ~10 ms between counter snapshots) set their own slower pace; the achieved rate is measured and reported as power_sensor_hz so a reader knows which power bands are physically meaningful.
FIELD_BANDS
Center frequencies (Hz) of the ten wave bands, ordered slowest→fastest and matched to BrainWave in Inner_World/brain_waves.py: infraslow, delta, theta, alpha, sigma, beta1, beta2, beta3, gamma, epsilon.
FIELD_REF_DIST_M 🔒
JITTER_ABS_FLOOR 🔒
Absolute turbulence floor: an RMS loop-period error equal to one full nominal period (CV = 1.0) is real turbulence no matter how ordinary it is on this host — below that, userspace scheduling noise is the machine’s own weather and does not heat the field.
JITTER_CLIMATE_TAU_S 🔒
Time constant (seconds) of the jitter-climate EMA the bath is measured against — slow enough that a scheduling storm registers as weather change, fast enough that a machine’s ordinary noise floor is learned rather than felt as stress forever.
LOOP_AREA_M2 🔒
Reference geometry for the Maxwell estimates: a die/package-scale current loop (~1 cm²) and a beside-the-chassis distance. These are order-of-magnitude assumptions — the current swing is measured, the geometry is not.
PROBE_PERIOD 🔒
Nominal probe period — ~250 Hz sampling. Bands through gamma sit below the 125 Hz Nyquist bound; epsilon is reported as 0.
RF_SAMPLE_DIV 🔒
Radio telemetry cadences inside the field-sampler thread, as divisors of its ~10 ms tick: /proc/net/wireless every ~250 ms (the NIC’s own stats are beacon-driven — they don’t refresh meaningfully faster), Bluetooth HCI counters every ~1 s (an hciconfig subprocess — kept off the 250 Hz loop).
RING_LEN 🔒
Samples held per channel (~4.1 s at 250 Hz — one infraslow cycle).
V_CORE_ASSUMED_V 🔒
Rail assumed for the carrier-current estimate when no voltage sensor is readable — a CPU-core-class rail.
WIRELESS_IRQ_NAMES 🔒
Wireless driver names matched against /proc/interrupts device labels — the radios’ attributable share of hardware event flux. A radio riding a shared xhci_hcd line is not listed by name and stays inside the aggregate count: attributed only where the kernel names it.
WORK_SLICE_ITERS 🔒
Iterations of the calibration work slice per unit of carrier modulation. Tuned so the slice is ~150-250 µs on this hardware — enough to measure, small enough to cost ~5% of one thread.

request_scheduling_boost 🔒
Ask RealtimeKit to lift this thread out of bulk SCHED_OTHER competition — the unprivileged scheduling boost (rtkit-daemon runs as root and grants down to its advertised MinNiceLevel for same-uid callers; real SCHED_FIFO is policy-denied on typical desktops). A 250 Hz loop that loses its slice measures its own starvation as substrate jitter and heats the field with it — asking first keeps the bath honest about the machine’s noise rather than the thread’s run-queue position. The request is made over a persistent system-bus connection kept in inner: rtkit revokes a grant whose requesting client has left the bus, which is why a one-shot dbus-send cannot do this job. An absent service or a refusal leaves scheduling as it was — degraded jitter, never a failure.
