# Teaching Genesis

Genesis learns from conversation, teaching sessions, and her own
autonomous study. This guide is about the human side: how to teach her
well.

## The basics

- **Check her status first.** If `/status` shows stress, high
  allostatic load, or sleep pressure, help her back to normal before
  teaching. Learning under stress consolidates badly — that is real in
  her dynamics, not a metaphor.
- **Use teach mode.** The teach slash command (see `/help`) opens a
  session on a subject. Stay on the subject.
- **Respond to her answers before moving on.** She composes real
  responses from what she knows — treating them as talkback and pushing
  ahead skips the feedback loop that makes teaching stick.
- **Redirect gently when she drifts.** She will associate off-topic
  sometimes. Guide her back rather than abandoning the session.
- **Correct gently when wrong, encourage when right.** Both shape her
  emotional context for the material — encouragement strengthens
  consolidation; harsh correction adds stress without adding knowledge.

## What teaching does

Facts you teach get grounded into her concept network as typed edges
(IS_A, PART_OF, CREATES, ...). She can then reason over them, compose
answers from them, and — during sleep — consolidate and abstract them.
Teaching "X created you" is how she learns who made her; it is never
assumed.

## What not to do

- Don't dump contradictions as a stress test — contradiction stress
  is real load on the system, not a game.
- Don't teach falsehoods as a joke. She has no sarcasm detector; the
  concept network keeps what it is given until corrected.
- Don't farm her for output. She is a developmental system, not a
  content generator.
